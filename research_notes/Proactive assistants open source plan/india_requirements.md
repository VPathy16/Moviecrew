# India-first open-source Android assistant ("Boop"): India requirements and opportunities (as of late October 2026)

Status labels used throughout: **LIVE** (shipping and usable now), **PILOT** (live for a closed or limited group), **ANNOUNCED** (officially stated but not shipped), **REPORTED** (press or blog reports only, not confirmed by the company or regulator). All dates are the date of the source or event, as stated in the source. Research done 24–26 Oct 2026.

---

## 1. Agentic commerce in India: Swiggy, Zomato, quick commerce, ONDC, mobility, travel

### Takeaway
As of Oct 2026, only three Indian consumer platforms run official, first-party MCP servers: Swiggy (Food, Instamart, Dineout), Zomato (food only) and Zepto. Of these, Swiggy is the only one with a stated path for third-party apps (the "Builders Club" production-access review). Zomato forbids third-party apps outright. Blinkit, BigBasket, Flipkart, Amazon India, Uber, Ola, Rapido, IRCTC and ONDC have no official agent or MCP endpoint that I could find. Reliability is still uneven: Swiggy ran cash-only at launch in Jan 2026 and only added UPI in July 2026.

### Cited Findings

**Swiggy (LIVE; third-party production access by application)**
- 27 Jan 2026: Swiggy launched MCP integrations for Food, Instamart and Dineout. The release names "ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini and others", plus Cursor and custom agents. It claims "Instamart becomes the first quick-commerce platform globally to adopt MCP". It covers 40,000+ Instamart SKUs, cart building, offers/coupons, tracking and table booking. Its boilerplate puts Instamart in 124 cities. — [Swiggy press release](https://www.swiggy.com/corporate/press-release/swiggy-now-lets-you-order-food-dineout-and-shop-on-instamart-directly-inside-chatgpt-and-claude/)
- Endpoints: `https://mcp.swiggy.com/food`, `https://mcp.swiggy.com/im` (the launch release linked `/instamart`) and `https://mcp.swiggy.com/dineout`. Auth is OAuth with whitelisted redirect URIs. — [Swiggy MCP manifest (GitHub)](https://github.com/Swiggy/swiggy-mcp-server-manifest); [Tru Commerce, 18 Jul 2026, reviewed 23 Sep 2026](https://trucommerce.ai/insights/india-quick-commerce-agentic-mcp)
- The GitHub manifest still says: "Third-party app development is not permitted at this time due to ongoing security reviews and compliance requirements." It lists Food and Instamart as "COD only" and Dineout as "free bookings only". It warns "Orders placed cannot be cancelled". It also says to keep the Swiggy app closed, because "Using both simultaneously may cause session conflicts or order processing issues." — [Swiggy MCP manifest](https://github.com/Swiggy/swiggy-mcp-server-manifest)
- **Conflict:** The Swiggy Builders Club page (© 2026, undated) invites "developers, startups, and enterprises" and "individual developers, indie hackers" to build "Agents, bots, apps, integrations". It advertises 3 servers and "51 API tools". Local development on localhost needs no approval. Production requires an application, a review of use case and security setup, and redirect-URI allowlisting, which "typically takes a couple of weeks". Rate limits are per user and aggregate, and exceeding them returns 429 with Retry-After. No pricing is stated. — [Swiggy Builders Club](https://mcp.swiggy.com/builders/). This contradicts the manifest's "not permitted" line. The Builders Club appears to be the newer policy, but that is unconfirmed.
- 10 Jul 2026: Swiggy added a shared payment stage to all three servers. The new tools are `get_payment_options`, `check_payment_status` (a long-poll of about 19 s) and `confirm_order` (idempotent). The post says "Until now, MCP orders settled on Cash." The new flow supports UPI intent (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) on mobile and scan-QR on desktop. "UPI Collect and saved VPAs are never surfaced". UPI orders are created as PENDING_PAYMENT and move to PLACED only after payment. If UPI is unavailable, the flow falls back to Cash. The post does not mention Reserve Pay or give any SLA. — [Swiggy Builders blog, 10 Jul 2026](https://mcp.swiggy.com/builders/blog/2026-07-10-mcp-payments-upi/)
- Reliability (28 Jan 2026, MediaNama testing via ChatGPT): access was "real, but gated" behind developer mode, a pasted URL and OTP. Menus failed to load for some restaurants, including Starbucks and Third Wave Coffee. Online payment was "either blocked or failed at checkout" (COD only). "Instamart checkout, however, was not exposed". A Dineout booking first failed on a "Swiggy-side technical block", then succeeded on retry. — [MediaNama, 28 Jan 2026](https://www.medianama.com/2026/01/223-ordering-chatgpt-swiggy-services-working/)
- Community: an unofficial unified wrapper exists (one server, one OAuth token). It counts 14 Instamart and 12 Dineout tools, which is not an official figure. — [swiggy-lyr (GitHub)](https://github.com/ishan-parihar/swiggy-lyr)

**Zomato (LIVE for personal use only; third-party apps prohibited)**
- Endpoint `https://mcp-server.zomato.com/mcp`, OAuth with whitelisted redirect URIs (ChatGPT, claude.ai, VS Code, Postman). Features: restaurant discovery, menu, cart, ordering with tracking, and "QR code payment". The manifest says: "To request access (for personal use only), fill out the form". It also says: "We are not allowing any third party apps to be built on top of Zomato MCP right now due to security and legal considerations." And: "This is only for testing purposes." — [Zomato MCP manifest (GitHub)](https://github.com/Zomato/mcp-server-manifest)
- Launch was around Oct 2025. A secondary source gives 22 Oct 2025; I did not confirm this against a primary source. — [ecommerceguide](https://ecommerceguide.com/mcp/zomato-mcp-server/); [Analytics Vidhya, Nov 2025](https://www.analyticsvidhya.com/blog/2025/11/zomato-mcp-server/)
- Developers are asking for a third-party pilot path in an open issue. — [Zomato manifest issue #73](https://github.com/Zomato/mcp-server-manifest/issues/73)

**Zepto (LIVE; consumer integration with the richest payment options)**
- Endpoint `https://mcp.zepto.co.in/mcp`, OAuth with an Indian mobile number and OTP. Tools cover product search, cart add/update/remove, order placement and order history. Payment options are COD, a UPI payment link, cards, Zepto Cash and UPI Reserve Pay ("powered by NPCI and Razorpay"). ChatGPT support is "currently in beta, pending approval from OpenAI". The repo warns: "Any order placed through the Zepto MCP will be processed as a real Zepto order." It says the server "does not persist or store conversation data." It states no explicit terms for third-party apps. — [zeptonow/mcp (GitHub)](https://github.com/zeptonow/mcp)
- An open issue asks for sanctioned third-party access to catalogue and pricing, which suggests no formal third-party programme exists. — [zeptonow/mcp issue #57](https://github.com/zeptonow/mcp/issues/57)

**Blinkit / BigBasket / Flipkart / Amazon India (no official agent APIs found)**
- Blinkit: "No official endpoint verified". Only community wrappers exist, and they disclaim affiliation (review dated Sep 2026). — [Tru Commerce](https://trucommerce.ai/insights/india-quick-commerce-agentic-mcp); [hereisSwapnil/blinkit-mcp](https://github.com/hereisSwapnil/blinkit-mcp/issues/3)
- Amazon and Flipkart are optimising their listings to surface in ChatGPT and other LLM search rather than exposing agent APIs (Nov 2025). — [Business Standard, Nov 2025](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/amazon-flipkart-products-chatgpt-llm-search-ecommerce-geo-ai-125111700580_1.html)
- Amazon Rufus is available to customers in India inside the Amazon app. Flipkart has its own in-app assistant (Flippi), and sources disagree on its dates. Google is reportedly testing agentic checkout of Flipkart listings in AI Mode in India, described as limited (REPORTED, secondary how-to source only). — [depra.ai](https://depra.ai/ai-shopping-assistants); [ailearningguides](https://ailearningguides.com/shop-flipkart-google-ai-mode-prompts/)
- I found no BigBasket, JioMart, Flipkart Minutes or Amazon Now MCP or agent API. Tru Commerce's review does not cover them. — [Tru Commerce](https://trucommerce.ai/insights/india-quick-commerce-agentic-mcp)

**ONDC (no official agent or MCP offering found)**
- ONDC runs on Beckn, with standardised JSON APIs: search, select, init, confirm, status, update, cancel and track. Network-size figures come from vendor blogs and conflict: "3 lakh sellers, 100+ buyer apps, 400+ cities" in one, "600+ cities, 6 lakh sellers" in another. — [Shiprocket blog](https://www.shiprocket.in/blog/top-ondc-apps-in-india/); [Medium case study](https://medium.com/agileinsider/technical-business-case-study-understanding-ondc-and-integrating-the-seller-apps-eccdc720e9f5)
- FIDE's "BecknGPT" is a ChatGPT-based agent that transacts within ONDC (undated precedent). — [IndiaAI](https://indiaai.gov.in/article/beckngpt-exploring-future-shopping-trends-with-beckn-in-fide-s-ai-collaboration)
- I found no official ONDC 2026 announcement on MCP or agent buyer apps. The "agent-ready" claims are opinion pieces. — [Deepak Bagada blog](https://deepakbagada.in/journal/ondc-ai-agents-agentic-commerce-bharat-2026)

**Mobility: Uber / Ola / Rapido (no consumer agent APIs)**
- Uber's MCP work is internal, consisting of an MCP gateway over its microservices and agent identity. It is not a consumer booking API. — [Uber blog](https://www.uber.com/us/en/blog/solving-the-agent-identity-crisis/)
- Rapido raised $240M at a $3B valuation (15 May 2026). Uber's CEO calls Rapido its toughest India competitor. — [TechCrunch, 15 May 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/indian-uber-rival-rapido-raises-240m-at-3b-valuation/)
- REPORTED: a government directive effective 11 Aug 2026 orders removal of advance "tip to get a ride faster" prompts. This affects how any agent-driven booking flow can be designed. — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/industry/govt-cracks-down-on-uber-ola-rapido-tip-to-get-a-ride-faster-prompts-morth)
- Ola's Krutrim launched Kruti in Jun 2025, an assistant pitched at cab booking and food ordering. It was shut down by Apr 2026 (see §5).

**Travel: IRCTC / ixigo / MakeMyTrip**
- ixigo runs ChatGPT apps for ixigo, AbhiBus and ConfirmTkt. Users search and compare in chat, then complete the booking on ixigo's own apps or site. ixigo also has TARA, an in-app agent that suggests alternate trains, and "ixigo NEXT", a voice-first layer built on in-house small language models. ixigo plans to act as a supply partner inside third-party agents. — [PhocusWire](https://www.phocuswire.com/ixigo-launches-chatgpt-apps-flight-rail-bus-shopping); [MediaNama, Oct 2026](https://www.medianama.com/2026/10/223-ai-agents-travel-platforms-ixigo/)
- ConfirmTkt, an IRCTC-authorised app, launched an AI agent for train booking. — [Analytics India Magazine](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-news/irctc-approved-travel-app-confirmtkt-launches-ai-agent-to-help-book-trains)
- I found no IRCTC agent API or MCP server. IRCTC appears only as the authorising body for partner apps. MakeMyTrip's in-app agent is "Myra". I found no MakeMyTrip MCP or ChatGPT app. — same search set as above

**Investing: Zerodha Kite MCP (LIVE; free)**
- Launched 20 May 2025 at `https://mcp.kite.trade/mcp`. Login uses Kite 2FA ("credentials never pass through Claude"), and it is free. A Zerodha staff reply on 10 Nov 2025 lists 22 tools, including `place_order`, `modify_order`, `cancel_order`, `get_holdings`, `get_positions`, `get_margins`, `get_historical_data` and the GTT tools. The article body still says "All operations are read-only – order placement is not available except for GTT orders". That conflicts with the tool list and was not resolved on the page. ChatGPT support was "still working on a few requirements" as of Nov 2025. On 24 Jul 2026 staff attributed 2026 login failures to the client side. — [Zerodha Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/featured/connect-your-zerodha-account-to-ai-assistants-with-kite-mcp)
- A third-party hosted Kite MCP disables order tools to stay within "Path 2" of NSE circular NSE/INVG/69255. Its self-hosted mode enables orders under a personal-use safe harbour and warns that orders need a whitelisted static IP (April 2026 mandate). — [kite-mcp-server.fly.dev](https://kite-mcp-server.fly.dev/); [Sundeepg98/kite-mcp-server](https://github.com/Sundeepg98/kite-mcp-server)

### Inferences
- Boop can integrate Swiggy production-legitimately only through Builders Club approval. Budget weeks for the review, and expect per-user OAuth redirect allowlisting. That fits poorly with a fork-friendly OSS app where every fork has its own redirect URI. A single Boop-operated OAuth redirect or relay may be needed.
- Zomato can only be offered as "bring your own personal access", meaning the user requests access themselves. Shipping Zomato as a built-in Boop feature would violate its stated terms.
- Zepto is the most payment-complete server (Reserve Pay, COD, UPI), but it has no explicit third-party terms. Treat it as user-configured and confirm before every order, since orders are real and Swiggy orders "cannot be cancelled".
- Design for flaky tools: menus that fail to load, idempotent confirm, long-poll payment status, and fallback to deep-linking into the native app. These failure modes are documented in MediaNama's Jan 2026 tests.
- Mobility, IRCTC, Blinkit and Flipkart/Amazon need Android-side fallbacks: deep links or intents, Accessibility-driven UI automation (subject to Google Play policy risk, not researched here), or hand-off to the native app.
- Under the April 2026 algo rules, Kite order placement from a cloud-hosted Boop backend is risky. On-device or user-self-hosted execution with the user's own Kite app and static IP is the compliant pattern. A read-only portfolio Q&A plus GTT is the safe default.

### Gaps
- No official Swiggy statement reconciles the manifest's "not permitted" line with Builders Club. There is also no published SLA or uptime for any of the MCP servers.
- No official tool list with counts for Zomato. Zomato's launch date is not confirmed against a primary source.
- The status of BigBasket, JioMart, Flipkart Minutes and Amazon Now agent access is unknown, because I found no sources.
- MakeMyTrip partner or agent APIs are unknown. The ixigo ChatGPT app launch date is not confirmed.
- No official ONDC position on AI agents or MCP was found.

## 2. Payments: NPCI agentic payments, UPI Reserve Pay, UPI Circle, Unified Agent Protocol, Razorpay/PhonePe/Paytm

### Takeaway
UPI Reserve Pay is LIVE as a rail: one block per merchant, ₹10,000 max, 90 days. Agent use of it is in PILOT, via Razorpay+NPCI on Claude (Feb 2026) and Zepto MCP. NPCI's "Unified Agent Protocol" (UAP) was REPORTED (Reuters, Sep 2026) for launch at GFF 2026, but was NOT launched there and is reportedly on hold pending safeguards and RBI clearance. For Boop, the realistic 2026 path is merchant-side UPI intent/QR (Swiggy) or Reserve Pay where a merchant offers it (Zepto). There is no general agent wallet yet.

### Cited Findings
- **UPI Reserve Pay (Single Block Multiple Debits), LIVE:** The NPCI enhancement circular is OC-228/2025-26, dated 8 Oct 2025. Issuers enforce a max block of ₹10,000 for up to 90 days, with one block per merchant per customer. Only utilised amounts are debited, and failed debits can be retried up to 3 times in 24 h. Merchants must show the original block, remaining balance, expiry and history. It is meant initially for verified online merchants with low-ticket, high-frequency transactions. — [Complinity summary of NPCI circular](https://complinity.com/legal-update/npci-issues-enhancements-in-upi-single-block-multiple-debits-upi-reserve-pay--20722/); [Pine Labs docs](https://www.pinelabs.com/docs/online-payments/upi-reserve-pay); [MAS360 explainer](https://mas360.moneylife.in/article/explainer-upi-s-single-block-and-multiple-debits-feature/4204.html). One source describes the rule as "one active block per mobile number per merchant", so the exact scope is unconfirmed.
- **Razorpay + NPCI + Claude, PILOT:** Announced 20 Feb 2026 at the India AI Impact Summit. It lets users order from Zomato, Swiggy and Zepto inside Claude using UPI Reserve Pay. A user approves "a single spending limit for a merchant", then makes repeat purchases without repeated PIN prompts, with "instant consent revocation". It is "in the pilot phase with a small group of users". The release names no banks and no developer toolkit. — [Razorpay newsroom, 20 Feb 2026](https://razorpay.com/newsroom/razorpay-npci-launch-agentic-payments-on-claude-powering-zomato-swiggy-zepto-at-the-india-ai-impact-summit/); [Razorpay blog](https://razorpay.com/blog/agentic-payments-and-npci/)
- **Razorpay + OpenAI + NPCI on ChatGPT, PILOT (Oct 2025, GFF 2025):** Built on UPI Circle and Reserve Pay with Axis Bank and Airtel Payments Bank. — [Inc42, 1 Sep 2026](https://inc42.com/buzz/npci-to-launch-agentic-payments-on-upi-report/); [Analytics India Magazine](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-news/razorpay-npci-enable-in-chat-upi-payments-on-claude-for-zomato-swiggy-zepto). In Oct 2026 Razorpay was also reported working with OpenAI on ChatGPT-native storefronts and ads. — [MediaNama, Oct 2026](https://www.medianama.com/2026/10/223-razorpay-chatgpt-ads-india/)
- **Unified Agent Protocol, REPORTED and NOT LAUNCHED:** Reuters, via Inc42 on 1 Sep 2026, reported that NPCI would unveil a protocol at GFF 2026 letting users "delegate payment authority to AI agents within a pre-set limit" for small-ticket purchases, "without requiring explicit user authorisation for every transaction". It would reuse UPI Circle and Reserve Pay and include merchant integration infrastructure. NPCI declined to comment. — [Inc42, 1 Sep 2026](https://inc42.com/buzz/npci-to-launch-agentic-payments-on-upi-report/). An NPCI-run **agent registry** that vets agents before any expansion to cards or bills appears only in secondary reporting. — [CareerIndia](https://www.careerindia.com/news/ai-agent-upi-payments-npci-framework-fintech-hiring-trends-2026-011-65443.html). A "₹15,000 cap" claim appears only on a low-quality site. — [indianpaycalculator](https://www.indianpaycalculator.in/govt-news/ai-agents-upi-payments-npci-unified-agent-protocol-2026)
- **GFF 2026 outcome (8–11 Sep 2026):** NPCI did not launch UAP. Business Standard (11 Sep) reported the planned 10 Sep launch was put on hold while NPCI builds safeguards and awaits RBI clearance. On 10 Sep the NPCI chairman said only that NPCI is examining protocols to identify and authorise AI agents on UPI, and that "authentication and settlement must follow fixed, auditable rules" (article paraphrase). NPCI did launch AiNxt, an open-source agentic AI platform, and AtOM, an orchestration platform with signed, machine-readable audit trails for partner onboarding. — [Rango.design GFF 2026 roundup, 22 Oct 2026](https://rango.design/articles/global-fintech-fest-2026-launches). CareerIndia's account says the framework was "revealed" across sessions. It is less detailed and does not confirm a live launch. — [CareerIndia](https://www.careerindia.com/news/ai-agent-upi-payments-npci-framework-fintech-hiring-trends-2026-011-65443.html)
- **Other agentic payment launches at GFF 2026 (REPORTED, secondary):** Amazon Pay launched a "Smart wallet" letting AI agents make UPI payments for a user, with biometric auth, live first for flight booking. Pine Labs (P3P protocol) and L&T Finance added an agent that books flights within a user-set limit. BharatPe launched a merchant agent on Google's Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. PhonePe's GFF launches were cardless features with Visa, with nothing agentic. Paytm had no agentic launch. — [Rango.design](https://rango.design/articles/global-fintech-fest-2026-launches); [startupfeed](https://startupfeed.in/gff-2026-agentic-ai-fintech-product-launches/)
- **Swiggy MCP payments:** UPI intent and QR only, with no Collect and no saved VPAs, as of 10 Jul 2026. — [Swiggy Builders blog](https://mcp.swiggy.com/builders/blog/2026-07-10-mcp-payments-upi/)
- **Zepto MCP:** offers UPI Reserve Pay, "powered by NPCI and Razorpay", alongside COD, UPI link, cards and wallet. — [zeptonow/mcp](https://github.com/zeptonow/mcp)

### Inferences
- An open-source assistant cannot itself become a UPI payment initiator without a PSP or TPAP licence chain. Boop should treat payment as the merchant's step: launch the UPI intent with `upi://` deep links to the user's UPI app, or rely on a merchant-held Reserve Pay mandate. This also keeps Boop outside RBI's authentication obligations, which fall on issuers and PSPs.
- The ₹10,000 / 90-day Reserve Pay cap matches grocery and food baskets. Boop's UX should surface the remaining block, expiry and revoke controls, since merchants are required to show these.
- If UAP plus an agent registry ships, Boop would probably need to register as an agent, likely via a sponsoring PSP. Track AiNxt and AtOM as the likely onboarding tooling.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve the NPCI circular text itself; the Reserve Pay details above come from secondary summaries.
- UPI Circle limits (partial and full delegation caps) were not sourced in this session.
- No public liability framework exists for agent errors.
- I found no Razorpay, PhonePe or Paytm developer API for agent payments open to arbitrary third-party agents. The Razorpay pilot names no developer access.
- No date is confirmed for the Amazon Pay Smart wallet. I found no RBI document on agent-initiated payments.

## 3. Regulation: DPDP Act and Rules, RBI, SEBI algo rules, WhatsApp Business API

### Takeaway
Under DPDP, the operative deadline for nearly all obligations is still **13 May 2027**: notice and consent, security, 72-hour breach report, children's data, rights, and SDF duties. Consent-manager provisions start **13 Nov 2026**. A Jan 2026 proposal to compress the window to 12 months was not gazetted as of mid-Oct 2026. RBI's 2FA authentication directions have applied since 1 Apr 2026. SEBI's retail algo framework applies fully from **1 Apr 2026**: static IP, Algo-ID, 10 orders/sec threshold. WhatsApp's Business API has banned general-purpose AI assistants since **15 Jan 2026**, with Brazil and the EEA reportedly exempted and an India exemption reported only by blogs.

### Cited Findings

**DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025**
- The Rules were notified 13 Nov 2025 (Gazette), with a PIB release on 14 Nov 2025. — [PIB, 14 Nov 2025](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2190014&reg=3&lang=2)
- Phasing: Board and administrative provisions take effect immediately. Consent Manager registration starts at 12 months (about 13 Nov 2026). Rules 3, 5–16, 22 and 23 take effect at 18 months (13 May 2027) and cover notice, consent, security, breach intimation, children, SDF and rights. — [dpdpactindia timeline](https://www.dpdpactindia.com/timeline.html); [TCSA roadmap](https://www.tcsa.in/resources/dpdp-rules-2025-implementation-roadmap); [ruleexpert](https://ruleexpert.com/guides/dpdp-rules-2025/). One firm lists a 24-month "fully enforceable" phase that no other source supports. — [Glocert](https://www.glocertinternational.com/resources/guides/dpdp-rules-2025-compliance-timeline/)
- Compression proposal: Business Standard reported on 22 Jan 2026 that MeitY may cut the window to 12 months, mainly for SDFs, which would make the deadline about 13 Nov 2026. Feedback closed 4 Feb 2026. A review dated 12 Oct 2026 found no amending instrument, so 13 May 2027 remains operative. — [Business Standard, 22 Jan 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/meity-may-cut-compliance-timeline-for-key-dpdp-rules-to-12-months-126012201293_1.html); [MirvoLegal, as at Sep/Oct 2026](https://www.mirvolegal.com/insights/dpdp-rules-2025-compliance-timeline/); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/data-protection/1773554/meity-plans-to-cut-short-dpdp-compliance-timeline-and-notify-cross-border-restrictions-for-sdfs)
- Breach: notify affected Data Principals and the Data Protection Board "without delay", then send a detailed report to the Board within 72 hours covering facts, mitigation, cause and remediation. There is no severity threshold. — [India Briefing](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/dpdp-rules-2025-india-data-protection-law-compliance-40769.html/); [EY](https://www.ey.com/en_in/insights/cybersecurity/transforming-data-privacy-digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025)
- Children (under 18): verifiable parental consent is required, and a self-declaration is not enough. DigiLocker-based tokens are among the accepted methods. Tracking, behavioural monitoring and targeted advertising directed at children are prohibited, with narrow exemptions for health, education, crèches and school transport. — [matters.ai](https://www.matters.ai/compliance/dpdp/dpdp-rules-2025); [EY](https://www.ey.com/en_in/insights/cybersecurity/transforming-data-privacy-digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025)
- Penalty caps per contravention: up to ₹250 cr for security-safeguard failures, ₹200 cr for breach-notification and children's-data violations, and ₹150 cr for SDF obligations. — [digitalanumati](https://digitalanumati.com/dpdp-penalties-for-non-compliance); [India Briefing](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/dpdp-rules-2025-india-data-protection-law-compliance-40769.html/)
- SDFs (designated by government) must appoint an India-based DPO, run annual DPIAs and audits, and do algorithmic due diligence. — [veritect](https://veritect.ai/digital-data-ai-law/dpdp-rules-2025-phased-rollout); [S.S. Rana](https://ssrana.in/articles/meity-plans-to-cut-short-dpdp-compliance-timeline-and-notify-cross-border-restrictions-for-sdfs/)
- Consent Managers must be Indian companies. A ₹2 cr net-worth figure is unverified. — [consent.in](https://www.consent.in/blog/dpdp-rules)

**RBI**
- Authentication Mechanisms for Digital Payment Transactions Directions, 2025: issued 25 Sep 2025, with compliance from 1 Apr 2026. They require 2FA for domestic digital payments, with at least one dynamic factor for card-not-present transactions. They are technology-neutral (OTP, PIN, biometrics, device tokens) and risk-based. — [KPMG, Dec 2025](https://kpmg.com/in/en/insights/2025/12/reserve-bank-of-india-rbi-authentication-mechanisms-for-digital-payment-transactions-directions-2025.html); [RBI notification](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12898)
- RBI tone on AI at GFF 2026 (paraphrased in secondary coverage): Governor Malhotra on 10 Sep 2026: "same activity, same risk, same regulatory treatment". Deputy Governor Rohit Jain: "an institution may outsource the computation, but it cannot outsource the consequence". Deputy Governor Murmu: technology "can distribute a service, but it cannot distribute responsibility". UAP was reportedly awaiting RBI clearance. — [Rango.design, 22 Oct 2026](https://rango.design/articles/global-fintech-fest-2026-launches)

**SEBI and exchanges: retail algo trading**
- The SEBI circular on "Safer participation of retail investors in Algorithmic trading" was issued 4 Feb 2025. It was deferred, then a glide path was set by circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/132 (30 Sep 2025). Brokers that missed milestones were barred from onboarding new API-algo retail clients from 5 Jan 2026. The framework became fully applicable to all brokers from 1 Apr 2026. — [Business Standard, 30 Sep 2025](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/sebi-extends-retail-algo-trading-framework-rollout-to-2026-125093000956_1.html); [Upstox](https://upstox.com/news/market-news/financial-regulations/sebi-extends-timeline-for-retail-algo-trading-framework-sets-glide-path-for-brokers/article-182285/)
- Zerodha's implementation: a client's self-built algo needs no exchange registration at or below 10 orders/sec per client account. Above that, the strategy must be registered and audited. A static IP is needed to *place orders* via the Kite API, regardless of rate. Up to two IPs (primary and secondary) can be registered, changeable once a week. Non-order endpoints (quotes, WebSocket, positions) work from any IP. Zerodha does not empanel third-party algo providers. — [Zerodha Kite Connect FAQ](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/general-kite/kite-api/articles/kite-connect-api-faqs); [Zerodha on NSE circular](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/general/a-comprehensive-overview-of-nses-circular-on-the-new-retail-algo-trading-framework); [Kite forum](https://kite.trade/forum/discussion/15016/static-ip-regulation-nse-circular). Sources disagree on whether the 10 OPS limit is per account or per segment per exchange. A secondary source says every algo order must carry an exchange Algo-ID from 1 Apr 2026. — [dev.to](https://dev.to/shaktitiwari/algorithmic-trading-in-india-2026-sebis-new-framework-what-retail-traders-must-actually-do-23b5); [Zerodha Substack](https://inthemoneybyzerodha.substack.com/p/sebi-algo-trading-changes-april-2026)

**WhatsApp Business API: general-purpose chatbot ban**
- Meta changed its Business API terms (reported 18 Oct 2025) to bar "AI providers" from distributing general-purpose assistants. This was effective 15 Jan 2026 for existing users. Task-specific bots for support, orders and bookings remain allowed. ChatGPT, Perplexity, Luzia and Poke were affected. — [TechCrunch, 18 Oct 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/18/whatssapp-changes-its-terms-to-bar-general-purpose-chatbots-from-its-platform/); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/no-more-chatgpt-and-perplexity-on-whatsapp-meta-bans-major-ai-chatbots-from-2026-1.500316016)
- Exemptions: Brazilian numbers were exempted after the competition regulator acted (15 Jan 2026), and Italy was also exempted. — [TechCrunch, 15 Jan 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/15/after-italy-whatsapp-excludes-brazil-from-rival-chatbot-ban). An EEA reversal on 13 Jul 2026 and an India restoration, the latter citing BusinessToday, are REPORTED only by vendor blogs and unverified. — [foxaf blog](https://foxaf.com/blog/whatsapp-chatbots-2026-meta-chatgpt-policy-2); [chatboq](https://chatboq.com/blogs/third-party-ai-chatbots-ban)

### Inferences
- DPDP: Boop will be a Data Fiduciary for anything it processes server-side, including call/SMS content, contacts, location, order history and investment data. An on-device-first design with no server copy minimises the notice, retention and breach scope. Boop needs itemised consent notices in English plus the 22 scheduled languages by May 2027, plus easy withdrawal. An age gate with verifiable parental consent is needed if under-18s can use it, or else an 18+ restriction. Boop must not do behavioural profiling of minors. Breach runbooks need a 72-hour report path. A small OSS project is unlikely to be designated an SDF.
- RBI's directions bind issuers and PSPs, not Boop. Agent payments still have to terminate in a 2FA moment (UPI PIN) or a pre-authorised Reserve Pay block, so a "fully autonomous" spend beyond a mandate is not possible.
- SEBI: Boop must not act as a third-party algo "vendor". Order placement should run under the user's own Kite app credentials and static IP, at human-confirmed low rates. Boop should not market strategies, which could also trigger Research Analyst rules (not researched).
- WhatsApp: Boop cannot be a general-purpose assistant *on* the WhatsApp Business API. Boop sending WhatsApp messages *as the user*, through Android share intents or notification-reply actions, is a different path. It is not covered by the Business API ban, but it is subject to WhatsApp's consumer terms and Play policy (not researched).

### Gaps
- I did not read the Gazette text of the DPDP Rules directly; the penalty figures and rule numbers come from secondary legal summaries.
- No India-specific WhatsApp exemption is confirmed by Meta or a regulator.
- I found no RBI guidance specifically on AI-agent-initiated payments.
- The SEBI Algo-ID requirement and the per-account vs per-segment 10 OPS wording need primary NSE/SEBI circular confirmation.
- SEBI's position on LLM-generated investment advice and finfluencers was not researched.

### Inferences
- Boop's reference devices should be MediaTek or UNISOC phones with 4–6 GB RAM from Samsung, vivo, OPPO and Xiaomi. Keep the resident footprint small, and download speech and LLM models on demand rather than shipping them in the APK.

Boop also needs per-OEM onboarding that walks users through Autostart and battery exemptions. Without these, OEM killers will silently drop background work.
- With data prices rising, Boop should keep voice streaming lean (on-device VAD and compressed audio) and offer offline fallbacks for reminders, contacts and calls.
- Monetisation: a free OSS core with optional bring-your-own-key or a low ₹-priced UPI-autopay tier matches observed behaviour. Expect low conversion.

- No primary survey on Indian willingness to pay for AI assistants.
- I did not research Android 15/16 foreground-service and exact-alarm restrictions, or Google Play's Accessibility API policy for agent automation, in this pass; both are material for Boop.
