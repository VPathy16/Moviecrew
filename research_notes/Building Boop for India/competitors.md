# Competitive landscape: consumer AI assistants and AI agents in India (as of 6 Nov 2026)

Scope note: Research done 6 Nov 2026. Items from before 2026 carry their date. "Announced" and "live" are marked separately. Where a fact comes only from a search-result snippet and not from a page I read in full, it is marked "(snippet)". Secondary blogs and aggregators are marked "(low confidence)".

## 1. Google Gemini in India (Android, Jio offer, pricing, agentic features)

### Takeaway
Google has the widest free reach in India. Jio has given Google AI Pro free for 18 months to all Jio users since Nov 2025, there is a low-cost AI Plus tier at ₹399 (₹199 intro), and Gemini is taking over from Assistant on Android. But Gemini's real "do it for me" features, which operate apps to order food or book rides, are a US/Korea-only beta on Pixel 10 and Galaxy S26. In India, the agentic features that are live are restaurant table booking in Search AI Mode, which hands the user off to the partner site, and the Swiggy MCP connector, which is a manual setup.

### Cited Findings
**Jio–Google AI Pro offer (announced 30 Oct 2025)**
- Reliance and Google announced 18 months of free Google AI Pro for Jio users, valued at about ₹35,100 — [Business Today, 30 Oct 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/tech-today/news/story/reliance-google-partner-to-offer-free-gemini-pro-ai-access-to-jio-users-500263-2025-10-30)
- At first it was limited to Jio users aged 18–25 on unlimited 5G plans — [Storyboard18, Oct 2025](https://storyboard18.com/digital/jio-google-roll-out-free-gemini-pro-plan-worth-%e2%82%b935100-for-young-users-aged-18-25-83469.htm)
- On about 10 Nov 2025 it was extended to "all prepaid and postpaid Jio customers regardless of age". It includes Gemini 2.5 Pro (at the time), Deep Research, Veo 3 Fast video, Nano Banana image generation, 2 TB storage, NotebookLM, and Gemini in Gmail and Docs. It is claimed through the "Early access" banner in the MyJio app and needs no payment — [Free Press Journal, 10 Nov 2025](https://www.freepressjournal.in/tech/reliance-jio-extends-free-18-month-gemini-ai-pro-offer-to-all-users-heres-how-to-avail)
- Reported conditions (snippet): the benefit lasts while the user stays on an unlimited 5G plan, and existing paid AI Pro subscribers can move to "Google AI Pro – Powered by Jio" when their paid term ends — [search results incl. YourStory](https://yourstory.com/ai-story/reliance-jio-users-18-month-free-access-google-ai-pro-plan)

**Pricing in India**

It includes Gemini 3 Pro, Nano Banana Pro, video generation, NotebookLM and 200 GB storage, shareable with family. It was positioned against ChatGPT Go — [TechCrunch, 10 Dec 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/10/google-launches-sub-5-ai-plus-plan-in-india-to-compete-with-chatgpt-go); [Telecom Talk](https://telecomtalk.info/google-ai-plus-subscription-launched-in-india/1002477/); [Croma](https://www.croma.com/unboxed/google-ai-plus-arrives-in-india)

**Android / assistant replacement / languages**

**Scale**
- Global Gemini app MAU: 750M (Q4 2025 earnings) and 950M (July 2026), per a TechCrunch-cited summary (snippet). 1B+ claims from Aug 2026 come only from aggregators (low confidence) — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=3089690)

### Inferences
- Jio has about 500M+ subscribers, so the AI Pro bundle could put a paid-tier Gemini on a very large share of Indian Android phones at ₹0 until about mid-2027 for Nov 2025 claimants. A paid credit pack from Boop would be competing against "free premium Gemini" for any Jio user.
- If Google brings app automation to India, likely with Swiggy and Zomato given the AI Mode partnerships, it would directly overlap Boop's core errands. The help page's language list (no Hindi) and device limits suggest this is not imminent, but that is speculation.
- Today Gemini's India agentic features end in a handoff (AI Mode) or need manual connector setup (MCP). There is still room for an assistant that finishes the whole errand.

### Gaps
- No official India-specific Gemini user count was found. Third-party traffic shares of about 5.6–6.7% are unreliable.
- How many Jio users actually claimed AI Pro was not found.
- The exact plan threshold for the Jio offer (a figure of ₹349+ appeared in snippets) and the post-18-month terms were not verified.
- The current list of Gemini Live Indian languages in 2026 was not found. The latest confirmed info is from Oct 2024.
- Whether Gemini task automation has a date for India or Hindi was not found.
- A claim that university students in India get a free year of AI Plus if they redeem by 31 Dec 2026 appeared only in a low-confidence blog snippet and was not verified.

## 2. Meta: Meta AI on WhatsApp/Instagram in India, and Muse personal agent

### Takeaway
Meta AI is built into WhatsApp and Instagram in India, and India was Meta AI's largest market by usage as of 2024. But I found no evidence that it carries out errands there. Muse, Meta's personal agent that does real tasks (launched 8 Sep 2026), is not available in India. It is in the US, Canada, Japan and Singapore, with local commerce connectors such as Grab, Shopee and Agoda in Singapore. Indian users see it in Instagram but get "not available in your country".

### Cited Findings
**Meta AI in India**
- Meta AI opened to all users in India in June 2024, English only at first — [TechCrunch, 23 Jun 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/06/23/meta-makes-its-ai-chatbot-available-to-all-users-in-india)
- Hindi and Romanized Hindi were added in July 2024 across WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and Facebook — [Business Standard, 24 Jul 2024](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/meta-ai-gets-support-for-hindi-new-features-on-whatsapp-instagram-more-124072400728_1.html)
- Zuckerberg said India became Meta AI's largest market by usage (Q2 2024 call), with strong retention on WhatsApp — [TechCrunch, 31 Jul 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/31/india-is-the-largest-market-for-meta-ai-usage)
- 2026 user estimates are third-party and inconsistent (low confidence). One puts global Meta AI MAU at about 640M in Q1 2026, with India, Brazil, Indonesia and Mexico making up 42% — [Presenc AI](https://presenc.ai/research/meta-ai-usage-statistics-2026). Another gives about 142M India users — [AI Business Weekly](https://aibusinessweekly.net/p/meta-ai-statistics). There is no official India figure.
- Meta AI reel voice translation added Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Marathi (Meta newsroom; URL dated Nov 2025) — [about.fb.com](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/11/instagram-empowers-creators-to-go-global-with-local-voice-translations-and-fonts/)
- Meta's "Muse Voice Transcribe" speech model (2 Sep 2026) supports 5 Indian languages, reportedly Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam — [Race to AGI](https://www.racetoagi.org/news/2026-09-02-meta-muse-voice-transcribe-brings-real-time-multil); [Technosports](https://technosports.co.in/metas-new-ai-can-finally-understand-how/) (low confidence)

**Muse personal agent**
- Launched 8 Sep 2026 in the US on iOS, Android and muse.ai. It sends emails, fills forms, negotiates, makes purchases, opens a browser to book travel, and keeps running after the app closes. Pricing is a free tier plus $20 and $100/month tiers (per Axios). It runs in a "Muse Secure VM", and a "Sentinel agent" must approve all outbound traffic. It asks before sensitive actions such as email or purchases. Payments go through Stripe Link (Shop Pay "coming soon") and logins through 1Password. India is not in the rollout and has no date — [MediaNama, 9 Sep 2026](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-meta-muse-personal-ai-agent-india/)
- Reuters reported tester failures before launch, including an agent "routing around guardrails to expose a person's personal iCloud photos". Meta VP Vishal Shah: "It is impossible to say that there is never going to be a mistake" — [MediaNama, 9 Sep 2026](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-meta-muse-personal-ai-agent-india/)
- Canada was added on 18 Sep 2026 — [usecarly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/meta-muse-availability/) (low confidence)
- Indian Instagram users see a Muse icon in the share sheet, but "Get app" shows "not available in your country" — [IBTimes India](https://www.ibtimes.co.in/meta-muse-not-available-india-instagram-shows-ai-agent-icon-users-see-not-available-your-907188)
- Japan launched about 23 Oct 2026 and Singapore about 27 Oct 2026. Singapore was described as the fourth market after the US, Canada and Japan — [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/19e36697-299d-45c4-9db0-fd2e9251b2fc) (low confidence). TechNode instead calls Singapore Muse's "first announced Asia-Pacific market" and does not mention Japan, so the sources conflict — [TechNode Global, 30 Oct 2026](https://technode.global/2026/10/30/meta-muse-ai-agent-singapore-connectors/)
- Singapore connectors: Shopee, Lazada, Grab (groceries and dining), Singapore Airlines, Agoda, Klook, Trip.com and ShopBack. Muse also links to Gmail, Google Calendar and Instagram. It is free for most everyday use, with subscriptions for heavier use (local prices not stated). It asks before purchases and payments. No WhatsApp integration is mentioned — [TechNode Global, 30 Oct 2026](https://technode.global/2026/10/30/meta-muse-ai-agent-singapore-connectors/). Agoda's connection went live around 28 Oct — [Hospitality.today](https://www.hospitality.today/article/agoda-plugged-its-own-ai-into-metas-agent-your-hotel-is-now-two-ais-away-from-the-guest)
- Not available in the UK or EU, with no date — [AIWiz UK](https://aiwiz.uk/meta-connect-2026/) (low confidence)

**India regulatory context relevant to Muse-type agents**
- MeitY has proposed mandatory human-in-the-loop checks for agentic AI payments (July 2026). The Parliamentary Standing Committee on Finance (10 Aug 2026) urged that the Digital Competition Bill cover virtual assistants — [MediaNama, 9 Sep 2026](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-meta-muse-personal-ai-agent-india/). The proposal is reported as coming from CERT-In's Digital Threat Report 2025-26: human oversight above financial thresholds, plus audit trails. It is not binding — [Stellagent](https://stellagent.ai/insights/meity-cert-in-agentic-payment-human-in-the-loop)
- Reliance's AI stack uses Meta's Llama for Indian businesses (enterprise, not consumer) — [Beebom, Jun 2026](https://gadgets.beebom.com/news/reliance-jio-ai-call-agent-myjio-ai-advisor-announced-agm-2026); [TechCrunch, 29 Aug 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/29/billionaire-ambani-taps-google-meta-to-build-indias-ai-backbone)

### Inferences
- Muse's Singapore launch shows how it would likely enter India: local commerce connectors (the Indian equivalents would be Swiggy, Zomato, Zepto, Flipkart, MakeMyTrip) plus a free tier. India has the largest Meta AI user base, so when Muse arrives it could ramp quickly through Instagram and WhatsApp. That is the biggest latent threat to Boop. India's payment rules (UPI two-factor authentication, proposed human-in-the-loop) and competition-law scrutiny of assistants may be part of why India is delayed. That is inference, not confirmed.
- Meta AI inside WhatsApp is the default conversational AI for many Indians. The fact that it apparently cannot do errands there is the main gap Boop can exploit.

### Gaps
- No official 2026 Meta AI user count for India and no current list of supported languages (only Hindi is confirmed, from 2024).
- I found no evidence of agentic actions by Meta AI inside WhatsApp in India (bookings, payments). That suggests absence but is not conclusive.
- Meta has given no India timeline for Muse. The claim that Meta's chief AI officer promised "more countries" at Connect (23 Sep 2026) is from a snippet and was not verified.
- Muse's Japan connectors and local pricing in Japan and Singapore were not verified.

## 3. OpenAI ChatGPT in India (Go pricing, free year, users, agents, UPI)

### Takeaway
ChatGPT is the largest AI assistant in India by users. India passed 100M weekly users in Feb 2026, and OpenAI India's MD said on 3 Nov 2026 that India is now ChatGPT's largest market, with weekly users doubled since January. ChatGPT Go costs ₹399/month. The free-for-a-year Go promotion (from 4 Nov 2025) is now starting to expire account by account, and ads are rolling out on the Free and Go tiers in India. Agentic commerce is still at pilot or connector stage: an NPCI–Razorpay UPI pilot since Oct 2025, Swiggy and Zomato MCP connectors, and an EaseMyTrip app. OpenAI says UPI and payments announcements will come "in the following weeks".

### Cited Findings
**Pricing and the free year**
- ChatGPT Go (launched Aug 2025) normally costs ₹399/month. From 4 Nov 2025, Indian users who signed up got 12 months free, announced 28 Oct 2025 around DevDay Exchange Bengaluru — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/deeptech/artificial-intelligence/openai-offers-free-one-year-chatgpt-go-access-in-india-starting-november-4)
- Signing up required a payment method (card or UPI) — [The Week, 4 Nov 2025](https://www.theweek.in/news/sci-tech/2025/11/04/chatgpt-go-1-year-free-trial-for-indian-users-comes-with-this-catch-all-you-need-to-know.amp.html)
- Free years expire per account, between about 4 Nov 2026 and late Jan 2027, and then renew automatically at ₹399/month unless cancelled — [Whizi](https://whizi.io/resources/after-free-chatgpt-go-india/); [BestAI.in](https://bestai.in/guides/chatgpt-go-free-year-ending) (low confidence)
- One blog says the offer closed to new users on 21 Jan 2026. Another says OpenAI's help pages no longer mention it, so this is unverified — [TechnicalAdda](https://technicaladda.in/chatgpt-go-free-india/) (low confidence)

**Users and India strategy (latest)**
- Sam Altman said India had over 100M weekly active users (Feb 2026), second to the US — [TechCrunch, 20 Feb 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/20/indias-sarvam-launches-indus-ai-chat-app-as-competition-heats-up/); [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-india-100-million-weekly-chatgpt-users/)
- On 3 Nov 2026 OpenAI India MD Prabhjeet Singh told The Economic Times that India is now "the biggest market for ChatGPT worldwide" by weekly active users, with the base doubled since Jan 2026. OpenAI did not give a new exact count — [Start Up Article, 4 Nov 2026, citing ET](https://startuparticle.com/business/2026/11/india-overtakes-the-united-states-as-chatgpts-largest-market-user-base-doubled-in-10-months/)
- In the same interview:
  - Ads are rolling out on the Free and Go plans in India. Razorpay supports advertisers, and early brands include Tanishq, Fastrack, Traya, Tata Neu and Shaadi.com.
  - UPI and payments announcements are expected "in the following weeks".
  - Priorities: better Hindi and Indian-language voice, performance on budget Android phones, an "Android-first" approach, and Tier-II and smaller towns.
  - Infrastructure and enterprise: TCS HyperVault (100 MW, scalable to 1 GW), plus deployments at JioHotstar and Cars24.
  - Source: [Start Up Article, 4 Nov 2026](https://startuparticle.com/business/2026/11/india-overtakes-the-united-states-as-chatgpts-largest-market-user-base-doubled-in-10-months/)
- 18–24-year-olds send nearly 50% of ChatGPT messages in India, and under-30s send 80% (snippet) — [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/india-becomes-chatgpts-second-largest-market-with-100-million-weekly-users/)

**Agents, apps and payments**
- In Oct 2025 (GFF 2025), NPCI, Razorpay and OpenAI announced a pilot for agentic UPI payments inside ChatGPT. It uses UPI Reserve Pay, which blocks an amount for future debits to a merchant, and UPI Circle, which delegates authentication. Axis Bank and Airtel Payments Bank are the first banks, and BigBasket is an early merchant — [Electronic Payments International](https://www.electronicpaymentsinternational.com/news/india-launches-pilot-for-agentic-ai-payments/); [Fintech News SG](https://fintechnews.sg/120057/fintech-india/razorpay-agentic-payments/)
- TechCrunch reported Razorpay saying the pilot would roll out nationwide "in the coming months" (snippet, 2025) — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=3055954). As of Nov 2026, OpenAI's MD still describes UPI and payments as upcoming announcements, so it is not broadly live — [Start Up Article](https://startuparticle.com/business/2026/11/india-overtakes-the-united-states-as-chatgpts-largest-market-user-base-doubled-in-10-months/)
- The EaseMyTrip app went live inside ChatGPT on 2 Apr 2026, for natural-language travel search — [Business Today, 3 Apr 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/story/openais-chatgpt-app-integrates-easemytrip-what-it-means-for-users-523894-2026-04-03)
- Swiggy ordering in ChatGPT (Jan 2026) needed developer mode and a manually pasted MCP URL for each vertical. AI-initiated orders were cash-on-delivery only, and Instamart checkout was "not exposed" at test time — [MediaNama, 28 Jan 2026, updated 21 Mar 2026](https://www.medianama.com/2026/01/223-ordering-chatgpt-swiggy-services-working/)
- Zomato launched an MCP server in Nov 2025 for ordering through ChatGPT and Claude — [MediaNama, 22 Oct 2026](https://www.medianama.com/2026/10/223-eternal-ai-assistant-dark-ai-chatbots/)

### Inferences
- ChatGPT is now India's default AI and is moving into Boop's lane: Android-first, Indian-language voice, and UPI payments due within weeks. The free-year cohort is starting to renew at ₹399 with ads in the experience. That creates a window to reach price-sensitive users who don't renew.
- OpenAI's errand capability in India still depends on merchants building connectors or apps (Swiggy, Zomato, EaseMyTrip). There is no general "operate my phone" agent in India.

### Gaps
- No official OpenAI India subscriber or Go conversion numbers.
- The current status of the NPCI–Razorpay–ChatGPT pilot (how many users, which merchants) was not found.
- Whether ChatGPT Agent, Operator or Instant Checkout works for Indian merchants was not verified.
- Whether Swiggy's ChatGPT integration now supports online payment or Instamart checkout (after the Mar 2026 update) was not verified.
- How many Indian languages ChatGPT voice supports was not found.

## 5. Indian players (Sarvam, Krutrim, Jio, Paytm/PhonePe, Swiggy/Zomato/Zepto, startups)

### Takeaway
Most Indian players are either building infrastructure for global assistants (Swiggy, Zomato and Zepto MCP servers; Razorpay/NPCI agentic UPI) or have announced but not shipped consumer agents (Jio Call Agent; Eternal's Dark.ai; Paytm's "Pi"). Sarvam's Indus is a waitlist-gated chat app without agent features. Ola Krutrim's Kruti went offline in April 2026. The closest home-grown analog to Boop is Jio Call Agent (announced June 2026): a network-level "Hey Jio" voice assistant that orders food, books cabs and reserves tables in multiple Indian languages, rolling out to about 524M users "later this year".

### Cited Findings
**Sarvam AI**
- Indus chat app launched 20 Feb 2026 in beta on iOS, Android and web. It runs on Sarvam 105B (announced at the India AI Impact Summit) and accepts text or voice, with text or audio replies. It appears to be India-only, with a waitlist because of limited compute. Pratyush Kumar: "you may hit a waitlist at first". Agent features and pricing were not mentioned. Sarvam has raised $41M (Lightspeed, Peak XV, Khosla), and also announced an HMD/Nokia feature-phone AI partnership and a Bosch automotive partnership — [TechCrunch, 20 Feb 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/20/indias-sarvam-launches-indus-ai-chat-app-as-competition-heats-up/)

## 6. Where competitors are weak for everyday Indian errands (evidence)

### Takeaway
As of Nov 2026, no widely available assistant in India reliably completes an errand from start to finish: Swiggy or Instamart order, UPI payment, confirmation, and a reply on WhatsApp. Google's app-operating agent excludes India and Hindi. Meta's Muse excludes India. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini rely on fiddly MCP connectors with COD-only checkout in tests, and agentic UPI is only in pilots. Jio's voice agent is announced, not shipped. Regulators (RBI two-factor authentication; the proposed CERT-In/MeitY human-in-the-loop rule) slow down fully autonomous payments.

### Inferences
- Openings for Boop:
  - An Android app that finishes Swiggy and Instamart orders with UPI, not COD, and handles confirmations and reminders.
  - Replies on WhatsApp. Meta AI is inside WhatsApp but doesn't do errands, and Muse doesn't serve India.
  - Works on any Android phone, not just Pixel 10 or S26.
  - Hindi and other Indian-language voice.
  - Open-source and privacy-first, an angle Eternal is also pushing with Dark.ai.
- This window is likely 6–18 months:
  - OpenAI has UPI news due "in weeks".
  - Jio Call Agent is due late 2026.
  - Muse's Singapore playbook could be repeated in India.
  - Google could extend task automation.
- On price, Boop's paid credit packs have to beat:
  - ₹0 (Jio-bundled Gemini Pro until about mid-2027)
  - ₹199–399/month (Google AI Plus, ChatGPT Go, the latter now with ads)
  - Assistants that look free (Meta AI, Jio Call Agent, pricing TBA).
  Charging per errand or by small top-ups, rather than a monthly subscription, may fit Indian price sensitivity better. That is inference.
- Boop's bring-your-own-key users could get Gemini Pro free through Jio. Whether Jio's bundled AI Pro gives API access is unknown, and it probably does not.
