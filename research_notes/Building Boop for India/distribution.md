# Distribution and go-to-market channels for a consumer AI assistant in India (as of Nov 2026)

Scope: channels for Boop, an open-source, Android-first launcher and assistant app for everyday errands, built by a small team. Research date: 6 Nov 2026. Many figures come from secondary blogs; these are flagged. Older data is marked with its year.

## 1. Telecom bundles (Jio, Airtel, Vi)

### Takeaway
The big telecom AI bundles of 2025–26 (Airtel–Perplexity, Jio–Google Gemini) went to well-funded global labs. Both are now closed to new claims, and neither side disclosed the deal terms or the number of activations. Third-party data shows these bundles produce huge, short-lived download spikes and weak paid conversion. I found no evidence that any startup has got a comparable telco deal, so for Boop this channel is not realistic in the near term.

### Cited Findings
**Airtel + Perplexity (announced 17 July 2025)**
- Airtel gave a free 12-month Perplexity Pro subscription to "every single of its 360mn customers" (mobile, Wi-Fi and DTH). — [Airtel press release, Jul 2025](https://www.airtel.in/press-release/07-2025/airtel-partners-with-perplexity-powers-every-single-of-its-360mn-customers-with-perplexity-pro/)
- Airtel valued the benefit at about ₹17,000. Customers claimed it in the Airtel Thanks app (Rewards section). New users, current subscribers and former subscribers were all eligible, but only in India. — [Airtel blog](https://www.airtel.in/blog/airtel-app/unlock-the-power-of-ai-airtel-offers-perplexity-pro-worth-%E2%82%B917000-at-no-extra-cost/); [Perplexity Help Center](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/11842322-perplexity-pro-airtel-promo)
- The claim window closed on 16 January 2026, about seven months after it opened. — [Superpower Daily, Aug/Sep 2026, citing TechCrunch 18 Aug 2026](https://superpowerdaily.com/posts/perplexity-s-airtel-giveaway-won-millions-of-users-now-it-has-to-prove-they-ll-pay)
- Sensor Tower figures, as reported by Superpower Daily:
  - 5.9M India downloads in the launch month, up 625% on June 2025.
  - About 56M India downloads during the claim window, more than 9x the prior seven months.
  - India MAU peaked in October 2025 at more than 5x the H1 2025 average of about 2.6M.
  - Feb–Jul 2026 downloads were about 3.3M, down more than 90%.

  — [Superpower Daily](https://superpowerdaily.com/posts/perplexity-s-airtel-giveaway-won-millions-of-users-now-it-has-to-prove-they-ll-pay)
- Appfigures, as reported by Superpower Daily: Perplexity's India revenue was $878K in Jan–Jul 2026, 16% more than all of 2025. July 2026 revenue was about $156K, against about $34K in January 2025. The data cannot show whether bundle recipients became payers. — [Superpower Daily](https://superpowerdaily.com/posts/perplexity-s-airtel-giveaway-won-millions-of-users-now-it-has-to-prove-they-ll-pay)
- In Q2 2025 (the quarter before and during the launch), Perplexity's India MAU grew 640% year on year (Sensor Tower). TechCrunch reported at the time that Perplexity had no notable in-app revenue from India. — [TechCrunch, Jul 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/17/perplexity-sees-india-as-a-shortcut-in-its-race-against-openai)
- Undisclosed: who pays whom, any revenue share, and the activation count. — [Superpower Daily](https://superpowerdaily.com/posts/perplexity-s-airtel-giveaway-won-millions-of-users-now-it-has-to-prove-they-ll-pay)

**Jio + Google (Gemini / Google AI Pro)**
- Jio offered 18 months of Google AI Pro, which it valued at ₹35,100. Eligibility required a Jio unlimited 5G plan of ₹349 or more. — [Jio offer page](https://www.jio.com/google-gemini-offer/)
- The claim window ran from 30 Oct 2025 to 29 Oct 2026. Eligibility started with ages 18–25 and was later opened to everyone 18 and over. Secondary reports say Jio's terms page read "no longer available" by 31 Oct 2026. After the 18 months, Google AI Pro costs ₹1,950 a month or ₹19,500 a year. These details come from secondary blogs. — [bestai.in](https://bestai.in/news/jio-gemini-pro-offer); [swapbiswas.com](https://swapbiswas.com/blog/jio-gemini-pro-offer/)
- One older deal blog (desidime) still describes the offer as open; it appears to predate the closure reports. — [desidime](https://www.desidime.com/news/jio-gemini-pro-offer-last-date-gemini-jio-offer-ends-soon)
- No activation count was found.

**OpenAI and Vi**
- OpenAI's free 12-month ChatGPT Go offer (Nov 2025) went direct to all Indian users on any network, not through a telco. Claims needed a card or UPI method, and the subscription auto-renews. Secondary sources say new claims closed around 21 Jan 2026. — [aiproducts.in](https://aiproducts.in/blog/chatgpt-free-airtel-users-which-sim/); [aiproducts.in](https://aiproducts.in/blog/jio-chatgpt-offer-explained/); [Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/chatgpt/articles/openai-offers-free-chatgpt-one-061709201.html)
- No AI bundle on Vi (Vodafone Idea) was found. OpenAI has no announced telco partnership in India. Source is a secondary blog. — [aiproducts.in](https://aiproducts.in/blog/chatgpt-free-airtel-users-which-sim/)

### Inferences
- These deals were customer-acquisition subsidies paid for by companies with billions in funding, plus a retention perk for the telco. A free, open-source app cannot offer a telco a "₹17,000 value" headline, so a Boop–telco deal of this kind is unrealistic before Boop has scale.
- The Perplexity data shows the pattern: about 56M downloads, then a more than 90% drop in downloads once the offer ended. Bundles buy installs, not retention. For an assistant whose value grows with daily use, a bundle without onboarding and habit-building would likely churn the same way.
- A more plausible telco route later is a smaller, non-exclusive "featured app" slot in a telco app's rewards or apps section (Airtel Thanks, MyJio). No evidence was found that telcos offer such slots to startups (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- No public deal terms (revenue share, per-activation payments, who pays) for either Airtel–Perplexity or Jio–Google.
- No official activation numbers for either bundle.
- No reports of any Indian startup getting a Jio, Airtel or Vi AI bundle. Vi's AI partnerships, if any, were not found.
- Whether telco app stores (MyJio, Airtel Thanks) accept paid or free startup listings was not found.

## 2. Phone-maker (OEM) preloads

### Takeaway
Preloads are real distribution in India, but the 2025–26 AI assistant preloads went to funded players: Sarvam's Indus on the HMD Vibe 2 5G (May 2026), and Perplexity on all new Motorola devices (2025). Deal economics are almost never published. One Indian OEM line (Lava's Virat V1) now markets itself as having no bloatware. Regulation is in flux: CCI/NCLAT rulings on Google preloads, and a disputed government preload mandate for the Sanchar Saathi app. For a small startup, OEM deals are a medium-term option at best, most plausible with smaller or Indian brands, or through the Indus Appstore preloaded on Xiaomi phones.

### Cited Findings
**AI assistant preloads, 2025–2026**
- HMD's Vibe 2 5G launched in India in May 2026 with Sarvam's "Indus" chatbot preinstalled. The partnership was announced at the India AI summit in New Delhi in February 2026.
  - Indus supports 22 Indic languages.
  - It has no offline mode and no device-level shortcut to invoke it.
  - TechCrunch frames the deal as cheap hardware plus a regional assistant, reaching users that English-first AI tools miss.

  — [TechCrunch, 21 May 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/21/finnish-phone-maker-hmd-bundles-indian-ai-chatbot-onto-new-smartphone-in-push-to-reach-local-market/)
- In April 2025, Perplexity announced a global partnership with Motorola, saying its app will come preinstalled on all new Motorola devices. Business Today tied it to the Razr 60 Ultra launch. — [Perplexity blog, 2025](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/announcing-our-global-partnership-with-motorola); [Business Today, Apr 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/news/story/motorola-announces-perplexity-ai-partnership-with-latest-razr-60-ultra-473570-2025-04-25)
- Moto AI combines Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot (including Copilot Vision) and Perplexity. — [Motorola News](https://motorolanews.com/motorola-unveils-microsoft-copilot-vision-built-into-moto-ai-turning-ones-camera-into-a-gateway-for-real-time-discovery/); [Android Central](https://www.androidcentral.com/apps-software/motorola-moto-ai)
- On 3 Nov 2026, Motorola agreed to bring Taboola's DeeperDive AI engine to the Moto G series. This is an ad/media company paying for on-device AI placement. — [Digitimes, 3 Nov 2026](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20261103PD228/motorola-advertising-smartphone-2026-gemini.html)
- Lava, an Indian brand, ships its own in-house assistant, "Vayu AI", which debuted on the Agni 4 and is billed as "system level AI". Lava's Virat V1 5G (July 2026) is marketed as having full Android 16 and no preinstalled bloatware. — [GSMArena](https://fo.gsmarena.com/lava_agni_4_launched_in_india-news-70373.php); [Tech Times, 24 Jul 2026](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/321518/20260724/lava-virat-v1-5g-launches-full-android-16-no-bloatware-india.htm)

**How preload deals work and what they cost**
- Xiaomi signed a multi-year deal with PhonePe to replace its GetApps store with PhonePe's Indus Appstore on phones sold in India. Google Play stays preinstalled. No payment terms were disclosed. — [TechCrunch, 13 Mar 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/13/xiaomi-to-preinstall-phonepes-app-store-on-smartphones-sold-in-india/)
- Glance (InMobi's lock-screen platform) is the reference case for India preload scale:
  - Most of its active users are on Xiaomi/POCO, Samsung, vivo and OPPO phones.
  - Counterpoint found that 70% of newly launched sub-$250 smartphones came preinstalled with Glance.
  - It had 140M active users in India in Q1 2021.

  All of these figures are from 2021. — [YourStory, Jul 2021](https://yourstory.com/2021/07/inmobi-glance-active-user-base-india-140-million); [Business Standard, 2021](https://www.business-standard.com/article/technology/lockscreen-platform-glance-s-active-user-base-in-india-hits-140mn-in-q1cy21-121071300635_1.html)
- A secondary case study says Xiaomi prices phones near cost (1–5% margin) and makes money from ads in preinstalled apps, paid themes and gaming partnerships. It is not audited data. — [BrandsAwareness](https://www.brandsawareness.com/technology/xiaomi-case-study-mi-india-story-rise-fall-and-reinvention/)
- No reliable published cost-per-preload or revenue-share figures were found for India.

**Regulatory constraints**
- CCI order (Oct 2022): Google was fined ₹1,337.76 crore. It was told not to tie Play Store licensing to preinstalling Search, Chrome, YouTube and other apps, and to let users uninstall its apps. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/google-cci-order-unfair-imposition-restricting-rival-apps); [FoneArena](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/389668/nclat-cci-rs-1337-crore-fine.html)
- NCLAT (Mar 2023) upheld the fine but set aside the direction on uninstalling preinstalled apps. It kept the rule that OEMs cannot be forced to preinstall Google's app bundle. — [Onmanorama, Mar 2023](https://www.onmanorama.com/news/business/2023/03/30/google-nclat-upholds-rs-1338-cr-fine-grants-partial-relief-four-grounds.amp.html)
- The CCI appealed the NCLAT ruling to the Supreme Court in 2023. — [MediaNama, Jun 2023](https://www.medianama.com/2023/06/223-google-anti-trust-cci-appeals-against-nclats-order/)
- Sanchar Saathi mandate (28 Nov 2025): the DoT gave phone makers 90 days to preinstall the state anti-fraud app, with a clause that users could not disable it. A later PIB statement softened this, saying the app's features are not disabled or restricted, and asked makers to push the app to existing devices via updates. Apple's policy reportedly bars preinstalling third-party or government apps before sale. — [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-india-smartphone-cybersecurity-order/); [Tech Wire Asia, Dec 2025](https://techwireasia.com/2025/12/india-requires-apple-and-other-phone-makers-to-preload-its-anti-fraud-app/); [GSMArena](https://www.gsmarena.com/sanchar_saathi_smartphone_pre_install_mandate_india-news-70506.php)

### Inferences
- Both 2025–26 assistant preloads involved companies with much more funding and leverage than Boop: Sarvam is a well-funded, government-backed model company, and Perplexity is a global lab. Smaller OEMs or brands that need differentiation (HMD, Lava, possibly Nothing or Indian ODM-backed brands) look like the most receptive targets. Lava building its own Vayu AI and selling "no bloatware" phones suggests it would want a deeply integrated, possibly white-label assistant rather than a paid third-party app.
- Boop's open-source, launcher-level design could be pitched to an OEM as a white-label or co-branded assistant or launcher, not a paid preload slot. That avoids needing a preload budget. This is speculative; no precedent was found.
- The Indus Appstore is preinstalled on Xiaomi phones in India, a large installed base. Listing there costs little and gives a second store alongside Play. Listing terms for Indus were not verified in this research.
- Sarvam's assistant and PhonePe's app store are both called "Indus". Keep the two apart in the final report.

### Gaps
- No published preload economics for India (cost per install or activation, flat fee per device, revenue share). Industry practice is opaque; the only indirect signal is the 2021 Glance coverage.
- No confirmation of any AI preload deals with Samsung, vivo, OPPO, realme or Nothing in India in 2025–26.
- Whether the Sanchar Saathi mandate was enforced, withdrawn or modified after the PIB statement could not be confirmed. Outcome of the CCI's Supreme Court appeal not found.
- No MeitY rule on uninstalling preinstalled apps or on preload security testing was found in this round. There were reports of such proposals in 2023, but they were not verified here.

## 3. WhatsApp as a channel

### Takeaway
WhatsApp cannot be Boop's main assistant interface in India. Since 15 Jan 2026, Meta's Business Solution terms bar "AI Providers", meaning general-purpose chatbots whose core product is AI. OpenAI and Microsoft pulled their WhatsApp assistants in January 2026. Meta paused the ban for 12 months in Europe only (March 2026), after EU and Italian antitrust action. No evidence was found of any India exemption. WhatsApp remains usable for:
- low-cost transactional or utility messages (about ₹0.115 each);
- support bots that are ancillary to a business service;
- free consumer-to-consumer sharing of referral links.

### Cited Findings
**Policy**
- Meta changed the WhatsApp Business API terms in October 2025 to bar general-purpose chatbots, effective 15 Jan 2026. Meta's position is that the API is for businesses serving customers, not for chatbot distribution. Businesses using AI to serve their own customers are unaffected. — [TechCrunch, 18 Oct 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/18/whatssapp-changes-its-terms-to-bar-general-purpose-chatbots-from-its-platform)
- New API users came under the rule from 15 Oct 2025, and existing users from 15 Jan 2026. Meta defines "AI Providers" as companies whose AI is "the core product rather than a supporting feature". Still allowed: FAQs, bookings, order tracking, support, notifications and sales bots that are ancillary to a business service. This is respond.io's paraphrase, not Meta's own text. — [respond.io](https://respond.io/blog/whatsapp-general-purpose-chatbots-ban)
- Coverage names ChatGPT, Perplexity and Copilot as affected, and says Meta AI is effectively the only general-purpose assistant left on WhatsApp. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/no-more-chatgpt-and-perplexity-on-whatsapp-meta-bans-major-ai-chatbots-from-2026-1.500316016); [TechRadar](https://techradar.com/ai-platforms-assistants/meta-will-ban-rival-ai-chatbots-from-whatsapp)
- OpenAI and Microsoft discontinued their WhatsApp assistants in January 2026. — [Digit, Nov 2026](https://digit.in/news/general/instinct-clarifies-whatsapp-outage-after-users-suspect-meta-has-blocked-its-ai-agent-founder-explains-why.html)
- Regulators pushed back on the ban:
  - Italy's competition authority intervened in December 2025.
  - The European Commission objected in February 2026, calling the ban anti-competitive.
  - On 5 March 2026, Meta said: "For the next 12 months, we'll support general-purpose AI chatbots using the WhatsApp Business API in Europe."

  The article does not say the pause covers India. — [MediaNama, 6 Mar 2026](https://www.medianama.com/2026/03/223-meta-pauses-block-ai-chatbots-whatsapp/)
- Poke.com co-founder Marvin von Hagen claimed that after the EU pause, his WhatsApp API cost per user rose from $0.13 to $11.04. This is unverified. — [MediaNama, Mar 2026](https://www.medianama.com/2026/03/223-meta-pauses-block-ai-chatbots-whatsapp/)
- WhatsApp introduced new terms for third-party AI agents in August 2026. No details were found. Instinct, a personal AI agent that books flights, makes reservations and orders groceries over WhatsApp and iMessage, was still operating on WhatsApp in November 2026. Its founder put annualised transaction volume near $1B. It competes with Meta's own personal agent, "Muse". — [Digit, Nov 2026](https://digit.in/news/general/instinct-clarifies-whatsapp-outage-after-users-suspect-meta-has-blocked-its-ai-agent-founder-explains-why.html)

**Indian AI services on WhatsApp**
- MediaNama names two India-based AI services that depend on WhatsApp:
  - Reliance's Haptik, an enterprise conversational AI service and therefore an allowed business use.
  - PuchAI, a multilingual consumer chatbot. When MediaNama tested it in March 2026 it returned no results, although its website's WhatsApp link was still live.

  — [MediaNama, Mar 2026](https://www.medianama.com/2026/03/223-meta-pauses-block-ai-chatbots-whatsapp/)

**Pricing, India**
- WhatsApp moved to per-message pricing in July 2025. Rates range from ₹0.11 (utility) to ₹2.3 (international authentication). — [MediaNama, Mar 2026](https://www.medianama.com/2026/03/223-meta-pauses-block-ai-chatbots-whatsapp/)
- 2026 India rates, before 18% GST and before the BSP's markup:
  - Marketing: about ₹0.8631 per message, up about 10% from ₹0.7846; about ₹1.02 with GST.
  - Utility and authentication: about ₹0.115, unchanged.
  - Utility volume discount only after 25M messages a month (₹0.1081).

  These are third-party blogs; check Meta's rate card. — [MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026); [dev.to](https://dev.to/xreach51/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-in-india-2026-every-layer-of-the-bill-12i7); [ChatMaxima](https://chatmaxima.com/whatsapp-api-pricing/india/)
- Service (customer-initiated) replies inside the 24-hour window were free. From 1 Oct 2026, the first 1,000 service messages per business number per month are free, and billable service messages after that are charged at the utility rate. Source is a third-party blog; not verified on Meta's site. — [amomic.in](https://amomic.in/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india)

### Inferences
- A WhatsApp-native "Boop bot" as the main product would likely fall under the AI Provider ban in India. Even if a carve-out exists under the August 2026 terms, Poke's claimed cost jump suggests Meta may price AI agents punitively.
- Compliant, cheap uses for Boop:
  1. Utility messages such as reminders or "your errand is done" confirmations, at about ₹0.115 each, or about ₹11,500 per 100K messages before GST and BSP fees.
  2. Ordinary consumer WhatsApp share links for referrals ("send Boop to family"). These cost nothing and are not governed by the Business API.
- Meta's own agent "Muse" and Meta AI inside WhatsApp are direct competitors that have privileged access to the channel.

### Gaps
- Meta's verbatim Business Solution Terms text and the August 2026 AI-agent terms were not retrieved. Meta's official India rate card (developers.facebook.com) was not fetched directly.
- No confirmation of whether India has any exemption or regulatory challenge (for example, a CCI case) on the AI Provider ban.
- Current status of PuchAI and other Indian consumer AI bots on WhatsApp not confirmed.

## 4. Play Store: discovery, ASO, install costs, policy

### Takeaway
Google Play's Accessibility API policy is the single biggest constraint on Boop's design. It forbids using AccessibilityService for "autonomous" agents that plan and carry out actions, and it explicitly says launchers and assistants are not accessibility tools. Deterministic, user-scripted automation is still allowed, with in-app disclosure and consent. Google is steering AI-agent integrations toward AppFunctions. Android installs in India are cheap, but estimates range from about ₹5 to ₹45 per install by category and source. No authoritative 2026 AppsFlyer India figure was found.

### Cited Findings
**Accessibility API policy (official Play Console Help)**
- "Any use of the Accessibility API that enables an app to autonomously initiate, plan, and execute actions or decisions" is strictly prohibited. Not prohibited: "deterministic, rule-based automation, where behavior follows a static, human-defined script." — [Google Play Console Help: Accessibility API](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10964491?hl=en)
- Only verified tools that set `isAccessibilityTool="true"` and whose core purpose is helping people with disabilities are exempt. The page says: "apps that are not accessibility tools are: antivirus software, automation tools, assistants, monitoring apps, cleaners, password managers, and launchers." It adds that "a general assistant that is voice-activated… that targets a large user population" does not qualify. — [Google Play Console Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10964491?hl=en)
- Apps that are not accessibility tools need:
  - a prominent in-app disclosure, not only in the store listing;
  - affirmative consent, such as a tap or checkbox;
  - a disclosure kept separate from other data-collection disclosures;
  - a Play Console permission declaration, required since 3 Nov 2021 for apps targeting API 31 and above.

  — [Google Play Console Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10964491?hl=en)
- A January 2026 summary of the update gives an "LLM agent that books, buys, messages, or changes settings" as an example of a banned use. Source is secondary. — [MyAppMonitor](https://myappmonitor.com/blog/google-play-accessibility-services-policy-update)

**Other Play policies**
- The July 2026 policy update reportedly extends the User Data policy to third-party AI integrations, making developers liable for the data practices of the AI APIs they embed. Partly secondary. — [Play Console policy announcement, 15 Jul 2026](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/17134731?hl=en-GB); [Algoramming](https://www.algoramming.com/blogs/google-play-ai-policy-compliance-apps)
- AppFunctions is described as the sanctioned route for AI-agent integration. Its policy says AppFunctions must not deceive or manipulate AI agents, bypass security, or interfere with other apps, and per the summary the policy takes effect 14 Apr 2027. The date comes from a search summary of the Play Policy Deadlines page and was not fetched directly. — [Play Policy Deadlines](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/table/12921780?hl=en)
- Google Play also publishes an October 2026 PolicyBytes update. Its content was not retrieved. — [daily.dev summary](https://daily.dev/posts/google-play-policybytes---october-2026-policy-updates-kprf04mi4)

**Install costs (India, Android)**
- Global average CPI in 2026: about $1.12 on Android and $2.24 on iOS, a compilation citing Adjust and AppsFlyer. Not India-specific. — [The Social Outline](https://thesocialoutline.com/blog/mobile-app-cpi-benchmarks-2026)
- India Android CPI estimates from third-party sources (methodologies unknown, figures differ by up to 10x):
  - Linkrunner: median $0.12 for casual games and $0.80 for fintech. — [Linkrunner](https://linkrunner.io/tools/cost-per-install-benchmark)
  - Coinis: $0.40 for casual games and $0.90 for non-gaming apps. — [Coinis](https://coinis.com/glossary/cost-per-install)
  - Gurob: average ₹25–45 on Android and ₹70–150 on iOS in 2026. — [Gurob](https://gurob.in/blog-app-install-cost-india-vertical)
  - Vmobify: non-incentivised CPI of ₹5–20 for most categories in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities. — [Vmobify](https://vmobify.com/blog/buy-android-app-installs)
- AppsFlyer Performance Index 17 (Q1 2026) reportedly puts Meta's global CPI at $1.11. Global and secondary. — [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/mobile-app-marketing-statistics-2026-install-data)

### Inferences
- If Boop drives other apps through AccessibilityService with an LLM choosing the steps (for example, "book a cab" executed by tapping through Uber or Ola), the Play Store version is very likely non-compliant. Options:
  1. Ship a Play version limited to user-confirmed, deterministic flows, intents, deep links and AppFunctions.
  2. Distribute the full agent build outside Play: F-Droid, GitHub releases, or the Indus Appstore. The Indus Appstore's own policy on this was not checked.
  3. Have the user confirm each step, so the agent is arguably not acting "autonomously". This is an interpretation risk.
- Launcher (HOME role) apps are allowed on Play. The accessibility-tool exemption explicitly excludes launchers and assistants, so the restriction comes from Accessibility, not from the HOME role.
- With non-incentivised installs at about ₹5–45, a ₹1 lakh test budget might buy roughly 2,000–20,000 installs. Assistants need deep permissions and onboarding, so cost per *activated* user (default launcher set, permissions granted) will be several times higher.

### Gaps
- Not retrieved: Play policy text on the default assistant handler, default HOME role, and the Notification Listener permission. From prior knowledge, which was not verified this round, Play's SMS/Call Log policy has a "default assistant" exception. This should be checked.
- No AppsFlyer or Adjust official India CPI benchmark for 2026, and none for the utility/productivity category. Play Store ASO data for India (keyword volumes, featuring) not found.

## 5. Organic and community channels

### Takeaway
Regional-language micro and nano creators are the most cost-efficient paid-organic lever found. 2026 rate cards put nano creators at roughly ₹1,000–12,000 per post. One industry report says regional micro creators get 2–3x the engagement of metro macro creators at about one-tenth the cost. One case study reports a ₹6.47 cost per install from a creator-led launch. No good sourced data was found on referral programs or campus ambassador programs for AI apps.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 influencer rate cards:
  - upGrowth (Sep 2026): nano creators ₹2,000–8,000 per post; micro creators ₹8,000–80,000.
  - Kofluence (Jul 2026): nano creators ₹1,000–12,000 per Instagram post or reel; micro creators ₹2,000–80,000.

  — [upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/influencer-marketing-pricing-india-2026/); [JigsawKraft summary](https://www.jigsawkraft.com/post/influencer-marketing-cost-in-india-complete-2026-pricing-guide)
- The Reelax Creator Economy Report (H1 2026) found regional micro influencers delivered 2–3x the engagement of metro macro influencers at roughly one-tenth the cost per post. ### Inferences
- Short Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi or Bengali reels showing one concrete errand Boop can do (bill reminders, reading a PDF aloud, booking help) suit the channel's economics. A ₹50,000–1 lakh pilot with 10–30 nano creators is within a small team's reach.
- WhatsApp family groups are the natural organic spread loop in India. A "share to WhatsApp" referral link costs nothing and needs no Business API (see section 3).
- Boop being open source opens free developer and community channels: GitHub, Hacker News and Reddit, Indian developer communities, FOSS United, college coding clubs. No sourced data was gathered on their effect.

### Gaps
- No sourced India data on referral program performance (for example, CRED, Paytm or PhonePe referral costs) or on college campus ambassador programs for consumer apps in 2025–26.
- No install benchmarks specific to Hindi or Tamil creators, and nothing specific to AI or assistant apps.

## 6. B2B2C options (banks, fintechs, employers, housing societies, platforms)

### Takeaway
Indian super-apps embed AI from frontier labs or well-funded local model companies rather than distributing third-party assistant apps:
- Paytm integrated Perplexity search (2025).
- Swiggy launched voice ordering with Sarvam (2026).
- Xiaomi preloads PhonePe's Indus Appstore (2025).

The realistic B2B2C opening for Boop is integration in the other direction: Boop as a client that calls partner platforms (deep links, ONDC, open APIs), not a platform distributing Boop. Employer, bank and housing-society channels were not evidenced in this research.

### Cited Findings
- On 27 Feb 2025, Paytm (One 97 Communications) disclosed a partnership in an exchange filing to bring Perplexity's AI search into the Paytm app. It covers local-language queries, financial literacy and everyday decisions. Full rollout status not confirmed. — [Business Today, Feb 2025](https://businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/paytm-joins-hands-with-us-based-company-perplexity-to-launch-ai-driven-search-466072-2025-02-27); [YourStory, Feb 2025](https://yourstory.com/2025/02/paytm-partners-perplexity-ai-powered-search-fintech-app)

### Inferences
- Platforms like Swiggy, PhonePe and Paytm partner with assistants that bring users or proprietary models (Perplexity, Sarvam, OpenAI). An early-stage open-source assistant has neither, so the near-term play is to *integrate* (deep links and intents into Swiggy, Zomato, PhonePe, UPI and ONDC network apps), not to be *distributed by* them.
- Banks, employers and housing societies (for example, apps like MyGate or NoBrokerHood) are plausible pilots: "Boop for your society or company" on staff or resident devices. They would need sales effort and data-protection paperwork under the DPDP Act. No precedent was found.
- Sarvam's Indus app is an aggregator that may compete with Boop for the same "assistant does errands in Indian languages" position, and it already has Swiggy and Razorpay.

### Gaps
- No examples found of Indian banks, employers or housing-society platforms distributing a third-party AI assistant to users.
- No deal terms (revenue share, per-user fees) for Paytm–Perplexity, Swiggy–Sarvam or OpenAI's India partners.
- Not checked: ONDC buyer-app onboarding as a channel, or UPI/NPCI rules for AI agents initiating payments (for example, UPI "agentic payments" pilots).
