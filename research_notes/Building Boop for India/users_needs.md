# Indian smartphone users and what they need from an errand-doing AI assistant (as of Nov 2026)

Scope note: research done 6 Nov 2026. Most figures come from news coverage of primary sources (TRAI, NPCI, IAMAI-Kantar, Counterpoint, Nokia, OpenAI, Meta). Only a few pages were opened in full: the Inc42 ChatGPT article and the Counterpoint Q3 2026 release. Everything else is from search-result extracts of the cited articles, so check exact figures against the primary release before quoting them in public. Older data is marked with its year.

## 1. Smartphone and internet base

### Takeaway
India has about 958M active internet users (2025) and about 1.08B broadband subscriptions (mid-2026). Most growth now comes from rural "Bharat", where 57% of users live. Data is heavy: about 31 GB per user per month. Devices are under price pressure: in Q3 2026, shipments of phones under Rs 20,000 nearly halved and people kept their phones longer. An assistant has to run on older, mid- and low-spec Android phones that stay in use for years.

### Cited Findings
**Internet users (IAMAI-Kantar)**
- The 2025 edition (released Jan 2026) counts about 958M active internet users, up about 8% YoY. Rural users number about 548M, roughly 57% of the total, and rural adoption is growing almost 4x faster than urban — [Business Today, Jan 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/indias-internet-user-base-tops-950-million-as-rural-growth-ai-drive-adoption-513353-2026-01-29); [YourStory, Jan 2026](https://yourstory.com/2026/01/indias-internet-user-base-crosses-950-million-2025-iamai-report)
- Same report: 588M users (61%) watch short video. **18% of internet users go online through someone else's mobile device, and about 80% of them are rural** — [Free Press Journal, Jan 2026](https://www.freepressjournal.in/tech/indias-internet-user-base-crosses-950-million-in-2025-rural-growth-ai-adoption-and-short-videos-drive-surge)
- Sample-size reports for the 2025 edition conflict: about 87,000 vs about 100,000 consumers. One account says the survey covered 400+ towns and 1,000+ villages — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/indias-internet-user-base-tops-950-million-as-rural-growth-ai-drive-adoption-513353-2026-01-29)
- The 2024 edition (released Jan 2025) counted 886M active users, 55% of them rural — [Business Standard, Jan 2025](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/india-to-exceed-900mn-internet-users-by-2025-125011600669_1.html)

**Subscriptions (TRAI)**
- June 2026: total telecom subscribers were 1.348B, up from 1.343B in May, and tele-density was 94.31% — [FoneArena, TRAI June 2026](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/488465/indias-telecom-subscriber-base-june-2026.html)
- Total base was about 1.35B in July 2026 — [Times of Oman / TRAI July](https://timesofoman.com/article/176144-indias-telecom-base-crosses-135-billion-in-july-as-broadband-mobile-subscriptions-rise-trai)
- Broadband subscribers passed 1.08B: 1,080.15M in May 2026, up from 1,073.44M in April. Wired broadband was only 47.40M and fixed wireless access (FWA) 17.97M, so broadband is overwhelmingly mobile — [IANS / TRAI](https://ianslive.in/indias-telecom-subscriber-base-rises-to-135-billion-in-june-broadband-users-cross-108-billion-trai--20260728140331); [TelecomLead, May 2026](https://telecomlead.com/?p=126427)
- 5G FWA subscribers reached 12.94M in June 2026, with rural slightly ahead of urban. Broadband market shares: Jio 48.94%, Airtel 34.94%, Vi 11.97% — [FoneArena](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/488465/indias-telecom-subscriber-base-june-2026.html)

**Data usage and cost**
- Nokia MBiT 2026: average mobile data use was just over **31 GB per user per month in 2025**, up from 27.5 GB in 2024 (18% CAGR over 5 years). 5G traffic grew 70% YoY to 12.9 EB/month, about 47% of total mobile traffic (about 27 EB/month) — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/technology/indias-average-monthly-mobile-data-usage-per-user-hits-31gb-in-2025-nokia-3950714); [Storyboard18](https://storyboard18.com/digital/indias-monthly-mobile-data-use-crosses-31gb-5g-traffic-surges-70-yoy-nokia-report-ws-l-93902.htm)
- Jio and Airtel dropped their 1 GB/day starter packs in 2025. For new Jio subscribers the cheapest plan is now Rs 299/month with 1.5 GB/day, about Rs 7/GB by my own arithmetic — [Business Standard, Aug 2025](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/jio-data-tariff-move-good-news-for-airtel-voda-idea-125081901256_1.html); [Angel One](https://oga-prod.angelone.in/news/market-updates/mukesh-ambani-s-reliance-jio-ends-1gb-day-plans-hikes-base-tariff-to-299)
- Analysts forecast a headline tariff hike in 2026 (Jefferies: about 15% around June 2026; Morgan Stanley: up to 20%). I found no confirmed across-the-board hike, only plan-level changes such as Jio cutting the validity of its Rs 195 pack — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/brand-marketing/mobile-tariffs-likely-to-rise-15-in-june-after-two-year-gap-report-87311.htm); [Digit](https://www.digit.in/news/telecom/telecom-tariff-hike-on-the-horizon-jio-airtel-vi-may-raise-plan-prices-by-up-to-10-pct.html)

**Devices (Counterpoint, Q3 2026)**
- India smartphone shipments fell **20% YoY in Q3 2026**, the lowest level in a decade. **Shipments under Rs 20,000 fell 47% YoY**. Causes were record-high memory prices and OEMs holding back channel inventory. Consumers "extended their replacement cycles", OEMs are "trading down specifications" to hold price points, more price hikes are expected after the festive sales, and the 2026 market is forecast to shrink about 13% — [Counterpoint Research](https://www.counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/india-smartphone-market-slumps-20-yoy-in-q3-sub-inr-20000-shipments-nearly-halve)
- Q3 2026 brand shares: Samsung 17% (back at #1), vivo 16%, OPPO 12%, Xiaomi 12%. Indian brands grew from 2% to 7% (Ai+ +306%, Lava +91%), and MediaTek chipsets held 45% — [Counterpoint Research](https://www.counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/india-smartphone-market-slumps-20-yoy-in-q3-sub-inr-20000-shipments-nearly-halve)
- Omdia put the Q3 2026 drop at 26%, to 35.8M units, steeper than Counterpoint's 20% (figure from a search extract of press coverage) — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/tech/indias-smartphone-market-hits-10-year-low-as-q3-shipments-fall-20-budget-phones-worst-hit)
- 4G phones are coming back: Counterpoint expects 4G shipments to grow 3% in 2026, lifting their share from 11% to 13%, because costlier components widen the price gap between 4G and 5G phones — [TelecomTalk](https://telecomtalk.info/indias-4g-phones-are-making-a-comeback/1012468/)
- CMR reported budget 5G phone shipments up more than 1,900% YoY in 2025 (headline only, not verified) — [FoneArena / CMR](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/475102/indian-budget-5g-smartphone-shipments-2025-cmr.html/amp)

### Inferences
- The installed base is ageing and getting more cost-constrained in 2026. Boop should expect mid- and low-spec Android phones that stay in use 3-4+ years. Heavy on-device models are unrealistic for most users, and a light client backed by a cloud model is the realistic default.
- With 31 GB/month average use, data cost for text/voice chat is not a blocker for typical users. Cost and lightweight design still matter for the rural and low-tariff tail, especially if tariffs rise.
- "18% go online through someone else's device" points to shared phones and proxy use. Phone-level identity and multi-profile or "acting for someone else" flows deserve design attention.

### Gaps
- I found no 2025-26 source for **Android's share of the Indian installed base** (commonly cited as about 95%). This needs a StatCounter or Counterpoint citation.
- I found no data on the **RAM/storage distribution** of the installed base. Counterpoint's Q3 release did not discuss RAM.
- I found no reliable total for **5G mobile users**; TRAI's 12.94M figure covers only 5G FWA.
- I found no official current price per GB. The Rs 7/GB figure is my own arithmetic.

## 2. Languages, literacy and voice-first behaviour

### Takeaway
Indic languages dominate. In the 2024 IAMAI data, 98% of internet users consumed content in Indian languages, and even 57% of urban users preferred vernacular content. India is Google's largest market for voice search, with about one in three queries spoken. An errand assistant needs voice input and Indic-language (and code-mixed) understanding from the start.

### Cited Findings
- IAMAI-Kantar 2024 (released Jan 2025): **870M users (98% of total) accessed the internet in Indic languages**, and **57% of urban users said they prefer vernacular content** — [The Week / PTI, Jan 2025](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/01/16/dcm51-biz-iamai-internet-usage-report.html); [Business Standard, Jan 2025](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/india-to-exceed-900mn-internet-users-by-2025-125011600669_1.html)
- Coverage of the same report says Indic-language adoption drove a "55% surge in rural internet users" — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/surge-in-indic-language-adoption-leads-to-55-surge-in-rural-internet-users-53632.htm)
- A Google executive (Prabhakar Raghavan) said **nearly a third of search queries from India are spoken**, close to 10x the US ratio of about 3-4% (the article is undated in the results and probably predates 2025) — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/about-third-search-queries-india-spoken-top-google-exec)
- Oct 2025: Google added 7 more Indian languages to AI Mode and launched Search Live (voice and camera) in India. It said India is already its **largest user base for both voice and visual search** — [C# Corner news](https://www.c-sharpcorner.com/news/google-expands-ai-mode-to-7-new-indian-languages-and-launches-search-live-in-india); [TelecomTalk](https://telecomtalk.info/india-key-for-ai-search-innovation-google/982674/)
- The share of Indian users doing daily voice searches is reportedly almost twice the global average (secondary report, older) — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/about-third-search-queries-india-spoken-top-google-exec)
- OpenAI India (Nov 2026) says it is expanding Indian-language capabilities to reach users beyond major cities and is prioritising **Android-first** product development — [Inc42, 3 Nov 2026](https://inc42.com/buzz/india-becomes-chatgpts-largest-market-globally-user-base-doubles-since-january/)

### Inferences
- Voice is a core input mode, not a nice-to-have. Boop should accept spoken requests and voice notes in Hindi and major regional languages. It should also confirm actions back in short, plain-language text or voice.
- Even urban, English-capable users often prefer vernacular content, so Hinglish/code-mixed understanding probably matters more than formal Hindi. This is not directly sourced; see Gaps.

### Gaps
- I found no 2025-26 figure for the **Indic-language breakdown in the IAMAI 2025 edition**. The 98% and 57% figures are from the 2024 edition.
- I found no source quantifying **Hinglish/code-mixed usage**, the **top languages online by share**, **literacy or typing difficulty**, or **WhatsApp voice-note volumes in India**.
- I found no 2025-26 Google figure for the share of voice queries in Indian languages.

## 3. WhatsApp-centric behaviour

### Takeaway
WhatsApp is India's default interface with businesses. Meta says 91% of online adults in India chat with a business every week, and in 2026 it launched AI agents for Indian SMBs, with UPI-in-chat promised. WhatsApp is both a channel Boop's users already live in and a direct competitor for "talk to a business" errands.

### Cited Findings
- WhatsApp's India user base is estimated at about 535.8M monthly users (DataReportal, Feb 2025, via a third-party aggregator). Other aggregators say 500M-550M+, and no official Meta India MAU figure was found — [Hyperleap (aggregator)](https://hyperleap.ai/blog/whatsapp-statistics-india-2026)
- In Feb 2024, Meta India's MD said close to 60% of WhatsApp users in India interact with businesses on the platform — [Business Standard, Feb 2024](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/meta-doubling-down-on-whatsapp-business-in-india-says-md-devanathan-124021901150_1.html)
- WhatsApp says **91% of online adults in India chat with a business every week**, cited at the May 2026 launch of WhatsApp **Business AI** for Indian SMBs. The AI is customisable with a business's own information, answers FAQs about products, pricing, discounts and shipping, and **UPI payments inside the chat are "coming soon"** — [Business Today, 7 May 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/story/whatsapp-launches-ai-powered-customer-support-for-small-businesses-in-india-upi-payments-coming-soon-530313-2026-05-07)
- 7 Jul 2026: Meta launched the **Meta Business Agent** on WhatsApp for Indian businesses at its Business Summit in Mumbai. Meta says more than 1M businesses use business agents and that business-consumer conversation threads across WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram exceed 1B per day — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/whatsapp-gets-gen-ai-powered-meta-business-agent-for-small-enterprise-owners-in-india-4065355)
- From 1 Aug 2026, Meta moved to token-based pricing (reported as $2 per 1M tokens) for businesses using WhatsApp's AI tools — [NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/science/meta-to-charge-whatsapp-business-users-2-per-1-million-tokens/tldr)
- The latest official global figure for the WhatsApp Business app is 200M+ MAU (June 2023) — [ChatMaxima (aggregator)](https://chatmaxima.com/blog/whatsapp-business-statistics-2026/)

### Inferences
- Users already treat chat as the way to transact with shops, so a chat-first errand assistant matches existing habits.
- Users will expect to share WhatsApp forwards, images and voice notes with Boop and to have Boop act on them, for example "pay this bill", "remind me" or "order this".
- Meta's Business AI and Business Agent are taking the business side of WhatsApp. Boop's differentiation is the consumer side: one agent acting across many apps for the user, not one business's bot.

### Gaps
- I found no official Meta India MAU, no data on WhatsApp group usage or voice-note share in India, and no India-specific WhatsApp Business app user count.
- The 91% figure comes from WhatsApp itself, reportedly from a Kantar study. Its methodology was not checked.

## 4. Everyday errands and app usage

### Takeaway
The everyday errand base is very large and very mobile. UPI handles about 23B transactions a month, mostly small payments to merchants. People also pay bills (Bharat BillPay 3B+ a year), book trains online (about 88% of reserved tickets, about 1.45M a day), use DigiLocker (720M+ registrations) and order through quick commerce (50-60M monthly users, mostly in metros). Food and grocery apps are concentrated in the top metros, while payments and government services reach everywhere.

### Cited Findings
**UPI**
- **July 2026: 23.66B UPI transactions**, the highest monthly volume in UPI's 10 years — [YourStory, Aug 2026](https://yourstory.com/2026/08/upi-completes-10-years-clocks-nearly-13000-fold-rise-transaction-volume)
- May 2026: 23.2B transactions worth nearly Rs 30 lakh crore, about 748M per day — [StartupTalky](https://startuptalky.com/upi-hits-record-23-2-billion-transactions-in-may-2026-transaction/)
- Oct 2025: 20.7B transactions worth Rs 27.28 lakh crore, value up 16% YoY. The single-day record was 754M transactions on 18 Oct 2025 — [Business Today, Nov 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/banking/story/upi-hits-record-754-mn-transactions-in-a-single-day-monthly-volume-surges-to-207-bn-in-october-500469-2025-11-01)
- Calendar 2025 (Worldline report): **228.5B UPI transactions (+33%)**, of which **P2M (person-to-merchant) was 143.82B (+34%)**, about 63% of volume by my arithmetic. **Bharat BillPay handled 3.05B payments worth Rs 14.84 trillion, up 40% and 93%** — [Lokmat Times / IANS](https://www.lokmattimes.com/business/upi-payments-surge-33-pc-to-2285-billion-transactions-in-2025-bharat-billpay-up-40-pc-1/amp); [Open Magazine](https://openthemagazine.com/india/indias-upi-boom-how-228-billion-transactions-signal-a-micro-payments-economy)
- Ticket sizes keep falling, and sources differ. The overall UPI average was Rs 1,293 in 2025 (vs Rs 1,478 in 2024) in one source and Rs 1,314 in another. The P2M average was about Rs 589-592 (Worldline 1H 2025: P2M down 8% to Rs 589) — [Worldline India Digital Payments Report 1H 2025 (PDF)](https://worldline.com/content/dam/worldline/local/en-in/documents/main-page11111/Worldline-India-Digital-Payment-Report-1H-2025-1.pdf); [Meetanshi (aggregator)](https://meetanshi.com/blog/upi-statistics/)
- About 85-86% of P2M transactions are under Rs 500 (Aug 2024 data; secondary explainer) — [Vision IAS](https://visionias.in/blog/current-affairs/upi-at-10-how-india-built-the-worlds-largest-real-time-payments-platform)
- Worldline says groceries, pharmacies, food and beverage, and utilities have "normalised" QR payments — [Worldline 1H 2025](https://worldline.com/content/dam/worldline/local/en-in/documents/main-page11111/Worldline-India-Digital-Payment-Report-1H-2025-1.pdf)

**Food delivery and quick commerce**
- Swiggy Q1 FY27 (quarter ended Jun 2026): platform average monthly transacting users (MTU) up 27.4% YoY to **27.5M**. **Instamart MTU was 13.5M**, up from about 13.3M in Q4 FY26 — [YourStory, Jul 2026](https://yourstory.com/2026/07/swiggy-losses-down-34-as-revenues-rise-37-in-q1-of-fy27)
- Food-delivery MTU of about 19.2M has been reported, but its segment attribution is unclear across sources — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/07/swiggy-losses-down-34-as-revenues-rise-37-in-q1-of-fy27)
- Redseer puts quick commerce at **50M+ average monthly transacting users across 250+ cities (CY26 estimate)**, with the **top 8 metros at about 70-75%**. A separate Redseer note says 60M+ in H1 2026. The two figures conflict, probably because of different time bases — [BW Retail World](https://bwretailworld.com/companies/category-mix-competition-defining-quick-commerces-evolution-shows-report); [FnB News](https://www.fnbnews.com/Food-Processing/redseer-report-highlights-quick-commerce-to-accelerate-indias-100-billion-packaged-fb-market-86364)
- A Google–Redseer projection expects quick commerce to pass 100M monthly transacting users by FY31, with metros providing about 60% of incremental growth (projection) — [BW Retail World](https://bwretailworld.com/sector/e-commerce-marketplaces/india-online-retail-set-to-cross-90-bn-in-2026)

**Travel and government services**
- **About 88% of reserved train tickets were booked online in FY26** (another report says about 89%). IRCTC averaged **14.53 lakh tickets/day in FY26**, up from 13.88 lakh in FY25, and peaked at 18.40 lakh on 19 Aug 2025 — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/tech/irctc-online-bookings-soar-88-of-reserved-train-tickets-now-digital-as-govt-strengthens-passenger-friendly-systems); [Lokmat Times](https://www.lokmattimes.com/business/online-train-ticket-bookings-surge-to-88-pc-in-fy26-govt/amp)
- **DigiLocker had 72.43 crore (724M) registered users** and UMANG 11.66 crore as of 31 Jul 2026 (Rajya Sabha reply) — [Free Press Journal, Aug 2026](https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/digilocker-crosses-7243-crore-users-umang-records-1166-crore-registrations-govt-informs-rajya-sabha)

### Inferences
- The highest-frequency errands are small UPI payments, recharges and bill payments, food and grocery orders, and train tickets. These are repetitive, low-value and rule-based ("pay the electricity bill every month", "recharge Mom's phone before it expires"), which suits an assistant that remembers and schedules.
- Many tasks are tedious because of steps rather than difficulty: OTPs, finding bill/consumer numbers, Tatkal timing, and comparing prices across Instamart, Blinkit and Zepto. Boop adds the most value by removing steps and keeping reminders. This is an inference; I found no survey ranking tedium.
- Swiggy/Instamart reach about 27.5M monthly users and quick commerce is 70-75% metro. Boop's flagship food and grocery integrations therefore serve urban users first, while UPI, bill payment, recharge and reminder errands are national.

### Gaps
- I did not find **October 2026 NPCI UPI figures**, which should be out by now. They need a direct check on npci.org.in.
- I found no 2025-26 data on mobile recharge volumes, cab-booking users (Uber, Ola, Rapido), bus ticketing, or Zomato/Blinkit MTU for the same period.
- I found **no survey identifying which phone errands Indians find most tedious**.

## 5. AI assistant adoption, trust and willingness to delegate

### Takeaway
India is now ChatGPT's largest market by weekly users, more than doubling from about 100M WAU in Feb 2026, and free bundles have made Gemini Pro and Perplexity Pro mass-market. Surveys show high generative-AI use (56-71%) with conditional trust, and privacy and consent are the main concerns. Agentic UPI payments exist only as a limited pilot, and I found no India-specific survey on willingness to let AI pay.

### Cited Findings
**Adoption**
- Feb 2026: Sam Altman said India had **100M+ weekly active ChatGPT users**, the second-largest market after the US, with students as a key driver — [TechCrunch, 15 Feb 2026](https://www.techcrunch.com/2026/02/15/india-has-100m-weekly-active-chatgpt-users-sam-altman-says/)
- 3 Nov 2026: OpenAI India MD Prabhjeet Singh said **India is now ChatGPT's largest market globally by weekly active users** and that the base had **doubled since January 2026**. Growth is credited to users "building ChatGPT into daily workflows". OpenAI said UPI/payments announcements are expected "in the coming weeks", that it is expanding ads in India to support free and low-price access, and that it is prioritising **Android-first** development and Indian languages — [Inc42, 3 Nov 2026](https://inc42.com/buzz/india-becomes-chatgpts-largest-market-globally-user-base-doubles-since-january/)

  - Checkout.com: 24% of consumers say they will never delegate purchases to AI — [GoCardless guide](https://gocardless.com/guides/posts/agentic-payment-adoption)
  - GoCardless UK: consumers trust their own bank most to run a payment agent, and AI-assistant brands ranked below "I wouldn't trust anyone yet" — [GoCardless guide](https://gocardless.com/guides/posts/agentic-payment-adoption)

### Inferences
- Indian users already have strong, free, Android-first general assistants. ChatGPT is about to add UPI, and Gemini Pro is bundled with Jio. Boop cannot win on general chat. It has to win on finishing errands across apps and on being open-source and privacy-respecting.
- The trust pattern is high adoption with weak confidence in how data is used and with output reliability. Combined with NPCI's Reserve Pay design, this suggests Boop should use spending caps the user sets, confirm every payment explicitly at first, and show a clear log of what it did. Local processing or open code is a credible selling point, given 61% see on-device as more private.
- ChatGPT's daily open rate in India (46% of MAU) shows assistants already have habitual daily use, so a daily errand and reminder loop fits existing behaviour.

## 6. Segments: students, professionals, small businesses, older users and family helpers

### Takeaway
The best launch segment is urban working professionals and students on Android. They already use AI daily, live where Swiggy/Instamart and quick commerce operate, and pay by UPI constantly. The most distinctive second segment is the "family manager": adult children who handle errands for parents and relatives. The evidence is that family is the main source of tech help for elders and that 18% of internet users go online through someone else's device. Kirana and small-business owners are already being targeted by Meta's WhatsApp Business AI.

### Inferences
**Ranking of segments for an errand assistant** (my judgment from the evidence above):
1. **Urban working professionals (25-40) on Android.** They are time-poor, order food and groceries often, pay many recurring bills, and already use AI daily. They also live where Boop's Swiggy/Instamart connectors work and can set up connected services themselves.
2. **Family managers.** These are the same professionals, but running errands for parents and relatives: recharges, electricity bills, medicine orders, reminders, train tickets. This segment is distinctive because general chatbots don't handle multi-person errands well. It also matches evidence that family is the main tech helper for elders and that proxy device use is common. Designs worth considering: "do this for Mom" profiles, shared reminders, and payments with caps and approvals.
3. **Students.** They have the highest AI adoption, but fewer paid errands and lower spending. They are good for reach and word of mouth but weaker for payment-heavy features.
4. **Small business and kirana owners.** The need is real (WhatsApp orders, UPI reconciliation), but Meta is serving this group directly inside WhatsApp, and the needs are business workflows rather than personal errands.
5. **Older users acting alone.** They are the most underserved, but low smartphone access and capability make them a poor direct-to-user segment at launch. They are better reached through segment 2.

Bharat (rural, Indic-first) users are the long-term growth pool, since 57% of users and the fastest growth are rural. For them, voice-first Indic support matters more than food and grocery connectors.
