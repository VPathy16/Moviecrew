# India: how small sellers sell and handle enquiries today, and the digital commerce rails an AI agent could plug into (as of 17 Nov 2026)

Reading notes for the report writer:
- Confidence labels (high / medium / low) are given per finding. "Vendor claim" means the number comes from a company that sells a WhatsApp/chatbot/CRM product, or from Meta about its own product; treat as marketing unless stated.
- Several figures could only be checked through search summaries of the source page rather than the full page. Those are marked "(via search summary)".
- Currency: 1 crore = 10 million; 1 lakh = 100,000.

## 1. How Indian micro/small businesses sell online and handle enquiries today

### Takeaway
WhatsApp is the default sales and enquiry channel for Indian small businesses (about 15 million on the WhatsApp Business app; consumers overwhelmingly chat with businesses), but almost every hard-sounding number on "enquiries per day", "hours spent replying" and "orders lost to slow replies" comes from vendors with something to sell; I found no independent, methodologically sound India survey on those points. The most important new fact is that Meta itself launched a free-to-set-up "Business AI" inside the WhatsApp Business app for Indian small businesses in May 2026, which does much of what a third-party seller agent would do.

### Cited Findings
- WhatsApp Business app is used by about 15 million small businesses in India (figure cited in press coverage of Meta; exact date not stated in the summary I saw, widely quoted since 2023). Confidence: medium (Meta-originated, undated). — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/how-whatsapp-business-is-bringing-indias-smes-to-the-digital-fold/) (via search summary)
- 91% of online adults in India chat with a business weekly (Kantar study, 2025, commissioned by/cited by Meta; no methodology published in the sources I saw). Confidence: medium. — [Meta Newsroom, 14 May 2026](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/05/introducing-business-ai-on-whatsapp-for-small-businesses-in-india/); also [afaqs, 16 Sep 2025](https://www.afaqs.com/news/media/whatsapp-sharpens-its-business-play-at-mumbai-summit-with-payments-ai-support-and-ads-in-status-10470432)
- Bain & Company / Meta "Win with Conversations" (published May 2024; survey of 7,800 users and 150 businesses in India, fielded Oct 2023, plus 25+ executive interviews): about 60% of surveyed users message a business account every week; over 50% strongly prefer conversational journeys for everyday use; about 90% of "non-savvy" digital users prefer to interact with SMBs for day-to-day needs via conversational platforms; about 70% of surveyed large enterprises already engage half their customer base on conversational platforms; only about 200 million of 650 million social-media-active Indians shop online. Confidence: medium-high for the survey facts (Meta-commissioned); the "450 million more shoppers" figure is a projection. — [Meta Newsroom, May 2024](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/05/genai-powered-conversational-commerce-to-fuel-business-growth-in-india/); [Business Today, 23 May 2024](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/conversational-commerce-revolution-gen-ai-fuels-business-growth-in-india-430584-2024-05-23)
- Conflict to note: Kantar 2025 says 91% of online adults chat with a business weekly; Bain/Meta Oct 2023 says about 60% message a business account weekly. Different samples, wording and years; both Meta-linked. — [Meta 2026](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/05/introducing-business-ai-on-whatsapp-for-small-businesses-in-india/); [Meta 2024](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/05/genai-powered-conversational-commerce-to-fuel-business-growth-in-india/)
- Older Meta-commissioned data: 70% of surveyed online adults in India prefer messaging over email or calls (Kantar, 2022); 91% of surveyed SMEs using digital tools said messaging apps are a very important way to connect with customers (Ipsos, 2022). Confidence: medium. — [YourStory, Sep 2023](https://yourstory.com/enterprise-story/2023/09/how-whatsapp-india-is-helping-businesses-of-all-sizes-build-a-digital-identity) (via search summary)
- Meta "Business AI on WhatsApp" for Indian small businesses (announced 14 May 2026, updated 26 May 2026): lives inside the WhatsApp Business app (Tools tab > "Your Business AI"), no coding or third-party tool; answers customers 24/7 on products, pricing, discounts, shipping; recommends products, captures leads, books appointments, "helps close sales"; learns from the business profile, catalog and uploaded documents; owner can take over any chat, adjust behaviour or switch it off; supports "all native Indian languages"; rolling out to "eligible" SMBs (criteria not stated); price not stated; Meta said UPI payments inside the AI chat would "start soon" (not live at announcement). Confidence: high that it launched; low on actual reach and quality (no independent data). — [Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/05/introducing-business-ai-on-whatsapp-for-small-businesses-in-india/)
- Testimonials in Meta's own announcement (vendor claim, not verified): one seller previously handled 60–70 customer queries a day and now closes 6–7 orders a day through AI-handled queries with a claimed 40% sales increase; another claims conversion of 80–90% after adding 24/7 support. These are the only per-seller "enquiries per day" figures I found from a named source. Confidence: low (hand-picked). — [Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/05/introducing-business-ai-on-whatsapp-for-small-businesses-in-india/)
- WhatsApp Business Summit, Mumbai, 16 Sep 2025: small businesses can share a payment QR with one tap and accept payments inside WhatsApp; the Business app and the Business Platform (API) can be used on the same number; in-app calling to businesses and voice-based "Business AI" support announced for larger businesses; ads in Status and promoted channels. Confidence: high. — [afaqs](https://www.afaqs.com/news/media/whatsapp-sharpens-its-business-play-at-mumbai-summit-with-payments-ai-support-and-ads-in-status-10470432)
- Response-time and lost-sales numbers are weak. A WhatsApp-CRM vendor blog attributes to "a 2023 LocalCircles survey" that over 60% of Indian SME owners lose at least one potential customer a week to slow replies on messaging; I could not locate the original LocalCircles report. A chatbot vendor reports 84% conversion when replies come within 5 minutes versus 36% at 30–60 minutes (its own tracking, n=3,247 leads). Another vendor estimates owners spend 4.5 hours a day on WhatsApp. A blog states 60% of Indian consumers expect a reply within an hour and labels the number itself "industry estimate, not independently verified". Confidence: low for all four. — [Groweon blog](https://www.groweon.com/blog/indian-smbs-lose-leads-without-whatsapp-crm/); [Botsense blog](https://botsenseio.blogspot.com/2026/02/whatsapp-api-provider-business-hyderabad.html); [Botsense Delhi guide](https://botsenseio.blogspot.com/2026/02/whatsapp-business-api-delhi-complete-guide-2025.html); [Hyperleap blog](https://hyperleap.ai/blog/smb-customer-messaging-expectations-2026) (all via search summary)
- Qualitative evidence for home-based women sellers: LEAD at Krea University (report dated April 2024) surveyed 167 women running unregistered home-based businesses in Rajasthan and Kerala; lack of skills is named as the main obstacle to using social media for selling; other field pieces describe WhatsApp as the first step to getting orders online in tier-2/3 cities, with selling through WhatsApp groups of about 250 members and Instagram/Facebook stories. Confidence: medium (small samples, qualitative). — [LEAD/IFMR report](https://ifmrlead.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Harnessing-the-Power-of-Social-Commerce-Report-April-2024.pdf); [Medium field study](https://medium.com/hccxb/from-screenshots-to-success-stories-tracing-the-social-media-usage-by-women-micro-entrepreneurs-dbb12bc256e2) (via search summary)
- Quick commerce is taking the grocery/daily-needs order flow that kirana shops and ONDC hoped for: quick commerce was about $10–11 billion GMV in 2025, 16–17% of e-commerce GMV, projected $65–70 billion by 2030 (Bain "How India Shops Online", latest edition as reported by trade press). Confidence: medium (secondary coverage of Bain). — [Branding in Asia](https://www.brandinginasia.com/how-india-shops-online-the-forces-shaping-indias-e-retail-market/) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- The behaviour is not in doubt: customers message small sellers on WhatsApp and expect a chat reply. What is unproven is the size of the pain (hours lost, orders lost). A founder should not quote the 60%/84%/4.5-hour numbers; they are sales copy.
- The single largest competitive fact for a "seller's agent" is that the platform owner now ships one for free inside the app sellers already use, in Indian languages, with UPI payments promised. A third-party agent must offer something Meta's does not: negotiation within owner-set limits, cross-channel coverage (Instagram, calls, marketplaces), order confirmation workflows, or being on the buyer's side.
- Meta's own testimonial of 60–70 queries a day converting to 6–7 orders implies roughly a 10% enquiry-to-order rate for one small seller, i.e. about 90% of chat effort produces no sale. That is consistent with a real time burden, but it is one hand-picked case.

### Gaps
- No independent India survey found on enquiries per day, hours spent replying, or share of orders lost to slow replies for micro sellers. The LocalCircles survey cited by vendors could not be verified.
- No adoption, retention or quality data on Meta's Business AI in India since the May 2026 launch; eligibility criteria and pricing not published in the sources read.
- No reliable split of small-seller orders by channel (WhatsApp vs Instagram DM vs phone vs marketplace).
- The Bain/Meta report PDF could not be parsed for response-time data.

## 2. Size and structure of the segment

### Takeaway
The segment is very large by count and very thin by ability to pay: 7.34 crore unincorporated (informal) enterprises, of which only about 27% use the internet at all, and 7.83 crore Udyam-registered MSMEs of which roughly 38–40% are women-led. Order values in the value segment are tiny (about Rs 265 on the largest value marketplace). Old social commerce forecasts ($16–20 billion by 2025) do not appear to have been met in the form predicted.

### Cited Findings
- Unincorporated non-agricultural enterprises: 7.34 crore in ASUSE 2023-24 (reference period Oct 2023–Sep 2024), up 12.84% from 6.50 crore in 2022-23. Confidence: high (MoSPI survey). — [Business Standard, Dec 2024](https://business-standard.com/economy/news/employment-up-10-unincorporated-establishments-rise-12-28-govt-survey-124122400965_1.html); [PIB factsheet](https://www.pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?Id=149090) (via search summary)
- Share of those establishments using the internet for business: 26.7% in 2023-24, up from 21.1% in 2022-23. Confidence: high. — [Drishti IAS summary of ASUSE](https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/annual-survey-of-unincorporated-sector-enterprises-2023-24) (via search summary)
- Internet use is highly uneven: 40.6% among urban male-owned establishments versus 9.4% among rural female-owned establishments (2023-24). Confidence: medium-high (academic analysis of ASUSE). — [CEDA, Ashoka University](https://ceda.ashoka.edu.in/from-ownership-to-operations-mapping-gender-inequality-in-indias-informal-enterprises/) (via search summary)
- Female-owned proprietary establishments: 26.2% in 2023-24 (22.9% in 2022-23); in unincorporated manufacturing, 58% of establishments have female proprietors (largely home-based work). Confidence: high. — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/economy-and-policy/women-led-enterprises-rise-in-informal-sector-manufacturing-employment-at-14-year-low); [Sakshi Post](https://www.sakshipost.com/news/women-owned-firms-rise-262pc-informal-sector-wages-jump-13pc-361845) (via search summary)
- Udyam registrations: 7.83 crore total MSMEs on Udyam and Udyam Assist, of which 3,07,42,621 (about 3.07 crore) women-led, as of 28 Feb 2026 (Rajya Sabha answer). Confidence: high. — [IBEF, Mar 2026](https://www.ibef.org/news/over-3-07-crore-women-led-enterprises-registered-on-udyam-till-feb-2026); [IANS](https://ianslive.in/over-307-crore-women-led-enterprises-registered-on-udyam-till-feb-2026-govt--20260309133559) (via search summary)
- Later, single-source figure: 3.69 crore women-owned registered MSMEs, about 38% of registrations, citing a government factsheet dated 15 Oct 2026. Confidence: low-medium (one outlet, factsheet not checked). — [The Peoples Board](https://www.thepeoplesboard.com/?p=22751) (via search summary)
- India e-retail: about $60 billion GMV in 2024 (Bain "How India Shops Online 2025"), about $65–66 billion in 2025 growing 19–21%, projected $170–190 billion by 2030; 290–300 million online shoppers in 2025; tier-2+ cities gave about 50% of incremental orders in 2025; "hyper-value" commerce rose from about 5% of e-retail GMV in 2021 to over 12% in 2024. Confidence: medium (secondary coverage of Bain). — [Adgully](https://archive.adgully.com/india-s-e-retail-market-poised-to-scale-to-170-190-bn-by-2030-report-157741.html); [Branding in Asia](https://www.brandinginasia.com/how-india-shops-online-the-forces-shaping-indias-e-retail-market/) (via search summary)
- Typical order value in the value segment: Meesho FY26 (year to March 2026) average order value Rs 265 (Rs 274 in FY25); 264 million annual transacting users (one source says 265 million); 2.67 billion orders; net merchandise value Rs 41,560 crore; 10.1 orders per user per year. Confidence: medium-high (listed company results via press; AOV from one source). — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/meesho-reports-43-growth-to-11371-crore-losses-narrow-sharply-on-ai-led-efficiency-gains); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/meesho-q3-fy26-net-loss-rises-13x-to-490-cr-despite-32-revenue-surge) (via search summary)
- Social commerce sizing is old and conflicting: Bain/Sequoia (Dec 2020) put it at $1.5–2 billion GMV in 2020, projecting $16–20 billion by 2025 and $60–70 billion by 2030; a market-research vendor (Ken Research) puts "India social e-commerce" at about $8.4 billion in 2025 rising to about $15.2 billion by 2031 (10.3% CAGR). Different definitions; neither is a measured actual. Confidence: low. — [Business Today, Dec 2020](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/corporate/story/social-commerce-to-reach-60-70-billion-in-revenue-by-2030-bain-co-281199-2020-12-10); [Ken Research](https://www.kenresearch.com/industry-reports/india-social-e-commerce-market.md) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- About 73% of informal enterprises do not use the internet for business at all; the reachable base for any chat agent is roughly 2 crore internet-using informal establishments plus formal small firms, and the 15 million WhatsApp Business app users is a reasonable proxy for the practical addressable base.
- With order values of a few hundred rupees and thin margins, a per-seller subscription has to be very small or tied to orders actually won; this matches the failure pattern in section 3.
- Women-led, home-based businesses are a large and growing share (26% of informal proprietorships; 58% in informal manufacturing), and they are the least online; assistance that works in their language and inside WhatsApp matters more for them than a new app.

### Gaps
- No current (2025–2026) credible measured figure for India social or conversational commerce GMV; no recent Redseer number found.
- No national count of home-based sellers operating through WhatsApp/Instagram.
- Typical order values for home food, tailoring, tutoring and local services were not found in any survey.

## 3. Existing tools, pricing, adoption, and failures among small-seller store builders and social commerce startups

### Takeaway
India's small-seller software wave of 2020–2022 mostly failed on willingness to pay and retention: the best-known store builders left the micro-seller segment for better-funded D2C brands, shut their products, or collapsed. WhatsApp Business API tools exist in large numbers but are built and priced for businesses that broadcast marketing messages, and Meta has raised per-message costs in 2026 while giving small businesses a free AI inside the app.

### Cited Findings
- Dukaan: began with online stores for micro-businesses (kiranas), moved to SMBs in 2021, then to D2C brands; laid off about 30% of staff in March 2023 (second layoff in about six months); CEO framed the move as going to "D2C brands and SMBs which are willing to pay for software tools", away from small kiranas "where monetisation was challenging"; in July 2023 it replaced about 90% of support staff with an AI chatbot. Confidence: high. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/exclusive-lightspeed-backed-dukaan-lays-off-30-workforce); [Business Insider India](https://www.businessinsider.in/business/news/retail-tech-platform-dukaan-lays-off-30-of-its-workforce-second-layoff-in-6-months/articleshow/98692209.cms); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2023/07/dukaan-replaces-90-support-staff-with-ai-chatbots) (via search summary)
- Bikayi (Sequoia and YC backed WhatsApp-store builder): revenue fell from about Rs 1.9 crore in April 2022 to under Rs 10 lakh in July 2022; seller exodus; allegations that salespeople forged seller signatures to sign kirana owners onto EMI-based subscription plans; an executive is quoted: "There are about 50 million SMBs in India but not more than 20,000 that can run online stores." Confidence: high on the reporting, medium on the quote's generality. — [Inc42 investigation](https://inc42.com/features/bikayi-in-disarray-startup-hit-by-fraud-allegations-seller-exodus/) (via search summary)
- Khatabook shut its store-builder product MyStore in November 2021 and later laid off 40+ staff in a restructuring; Inc42's analysis concluded India's small sellers "still cannot offer great lifetime value as customers of tech products". Confidence: high. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/khatabook-fires-over-40-employees-restructuring-exercise/amp) (via search summary)
- WhatsApp Business Platform (API) pricing: Meta moved from per-conversation to per-delivered-template-message billing on 1 July 2025. India rates reported for 2026 conflict: marketing template about Rs 0.86 (ex-GST, from 1 Jan 2026, up about 10% from about Rs 0.78) per one provider vs Rs 1.09 per another (may include markup or be stale); utility/authentication about Rs 0.115 vs Rs 0.145. Service (customer-initiated, within 24 hours) replies have been free; providers report that from 1 Oct 2026 Meta began charging for utility templates inside the 24-hour window and, per one source, service messages beyond 1,000 a month per number at Rs 0.145. Confidence: medium on the model, low on exact rupee rates (all from providers; check Meta's rate card). — [Monty Mobile](https://montymobile.com/blogs/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-in-india-inr-rates-gst-and-the-2026-currency-migration-deadline); [AiSensy](https://m.aisensy.com/blog/whatsapp-per-message-pricing-update-effective-january-1-2026/); [baat.ai](https://baat.ai/blog/whatsapp-pricing-per-message) (via search summary)
- Conversational-commerce providers already active in India include Gupshup (launched "India's first conversational buyer app" on ONDC over WhatsApp, June 2024, beta, food ordering first), plus API providers such as Interakt, AiSensy and Wati (named in the provider pricing posts above). Confidence: high on existence; subscription prices not verified. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/gupshup-rolls-out-indias-first-conversational-buyer-app-on-ondc)
- Meta's own Business AI in the WhatsApp Business app (May 2026) and Flipkart's voice-enabled WhatsApp ordering bot for small retailers built with Sarvam AI (2026) show large players moving into AI chat for small merchants. Confidence: high on launch. — [Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/05/introducing-business-ai-on-whatsapp-for-small-businesses-in-india/); [MIT Sloan Management Review Middle East](https://www.mitsloanme.com/?p=9740) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- The repeated lesson is that micro sellers will not pay a recurring software fee and field-sales acquisition does not pay back; survivors moved up to D2C brands. Any agent for micro sellers needs near-zero acquisition cost (spreads inside WhatsApp, word of mouth) and pricing tied to money earned, or it must be paid for by someone else (the buyer side, a bank, a marketplace).
- An agent on the WhatsApp API pays Meta little or nothing for replying to customer-initiated chats but does pay for outbound templates; the October 2026 pricing change narrows the "free reply" window economics. This needs checking against Meta's official rate card before modelling costs.
- An agent that works on the WhatsApp Business app (not API) number is constrained by Meta's rules; the coexistence feature (app plus API on one number, Sep 2025) is the practical route for small sellers who will not give up their existing number.

### Gaps
- Dukaan's current (2025–2026) status, revenue and customer count not found.
- Subscription plan prices, customer counts and churn for Interakt, AiSensy, Wati and similar not verified from their own pages.
- No independent churn or retention data for any India small-seller SaaS.
- Did not find specific India AI sales-assistant startups for micro sellers with verified traction.

## 4. ONDC and the Beckn protocol

### Takeaway
ONDC is alive but is not where small retail sellers are winning: headline volume (about 9 million transactions a month in June 2026; 500 million cumulative by July 2026) is driven by mobility, transit ticketing and logistics, while retail orders fell after incentives were cut in late 2024 and key buyer apps pulled back. Beckn is technically usable by an agent (community Beckn–MCP bridge, Google-backed Beckn Onix with agent kit), but I found no official 2026 Beckn or ONDC agent standard and no evidence of consumer demand arriving through it for small sellers.

### Cited Findings
- What it is: ONDC is a government-backed (DPIIT) open network launched in 2022, built on the Beckn protocol, in which separate buyer apps, seller apps and logistics providers interoperate; Beckn was conceived by Nandan Nilekani, Pramod Varma and Sujith Nair, with FIDE (formerly Beckn Foundation) as author of the specification. Confidence: high. — [Analytics India Magazine](https://analyticsindiamag.com/when-two-ai-agents-communicate-beckn-can-be-the-contracting-infrastructure/); [Cross-Border Magazine, 7 Aug 2026](https://cross-border-magazine.com/ondc-500-million-transactions-india/)
- Scale: about 200,000 transactions in FY2022-23; 218 million in FY2025-26; about 9 million a month as of June 2026; over 500 million cumulative by July 2026 (per DPIIT); about 500,000 sellers (June 2026), "over 200,000 active retail merchants" (July 2026); over 1 million service providers across mobility and logistics; about 1,000 cities. Confidence: medium-high (government figures via trade press). — [Cross-Border Magazine](https://cross-border-magazine.com/ondc-500-million-transactions-india/)
- Conflicting figure: another report says "more than 1.2 crore orders per month" and "over six lakh sellers"; date and counting basis unclear. — [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/ondc-onboards-over-6-lakh-sellers-enables-1-2-crore-orders-per-month-3090094) (via search summary)
- Mix: public transport ticketing alone was over 370,000 journeys a day through 35+ buyer apps in June 2026 (i.e. roughly 11 million a month if sustained, which sits awkwardly with the "9 million a month" total; counting bases may differ); logistics has 50+ hyperlocal providers and 60,000+ merchants; mobility orders grew from 3.6 million (Apr 2024) to 9.4 million (Apr 2025). Confidence: medium. — [Cross-Border Magazine](https://cross-border-magazine.com/ondc-500-million-transactions-india/); [Business Standard, May 2025](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/ondc-ecommerce-failure-struggles-india-125050501101_1.html) (via search summary)
- Retail decline: retail orders peaked at 6.5 million a month in Oct 2024 and fell to 4.6 million in Feb 2025 (10-month low); grocery about 1.8 million in Feb 2025 with a shrinking share; food delivery flat since its 2024 peak; an ONDC source called retail "a sob story". Confidence: high for 2024–25; no 2026 retail-only data found. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=509526)
- Incentives withdrawn: per-participant incentives cut from a peak of Rs 3 crore (July 2024) to Rs 30 lakh (Dec 2024); earlier, discounts were capped at Rs 100 per order; from late 2024 a network fee of Rs 1.50 per transaction above Rs 250 is charged on the seller side. Confidence: high. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=509526); [Inc42 on discount cap](https://inc42.com/buzz/ondc-limits-discounts-inr-100-per-order-new-incentive-regime)
- Exits and leadership: PhonePe's Pincode opted out of ONDC, reportedly because buyer-side economics were unsustainable; buyer apps (Paytm, Magicpin, Ola) cut consumer discounts; CEO T. Koshy and the chief business officer left in 2025; Vibhor Jain was made acting CEO. Confidence: high. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=509526); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/start-up/news/ondc-appoints-vibhor-jain-as-acting-ceo-following-thampy-koshys-exit)
- Why retail struggled (reported reasons): quick commerce (Blinkit, Instamart, Zepto raised about $3.5 billion in 2024) took the grocery use case; once incentives shrank buyer apps could not fund discounts; low commissions (about 5% on food vs 30–40% at Swiggy/Zomato) left buyer apps without a business. Confidence: medium-high. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=509526); [Finshots](https://finshots.in/archive/has-the-ondc-experiment-failed-nrai-rapido-t-koshy-swiggy-zomato-food-delivery/)
- Cost structure for a seller is not a single fee and published numbers conflict: a 2022 report cites a 3% cap on buyer-app commission with seller-app commission of 10–15%; a 2023 venture primer illustrates a Rs 15 buyer finder fee plus Rs 10 seller-app commission plus logistics, or 5–10% all-in; 2026 promotional blogs quote 3–10% versus 18–40% on large marketplaces; pricing transparency is described as a known weak point. Confidence: low-medium (old or promotional sources). — [Business Today, Sep 2022](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/economy/story/hows-ondc-solving-small-sellers-discoverability-and-commission-concerns-on-e-commerce-348124-2022-09-25); [Kalaari primer, 2023](https://kalaari.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/ONDC-Primer-%E2%80%93-Final-Full-Report.pdf); [eChai](https://echai.ventures/d2c/d2c-ondc/ondc-costs-margins-vs-amazon-flipkart) (via search summary)
- Government support continues: the MSME TEAM scheme has Rs 2.77 billion approved for FY2024-25 to FY2026-27 to onboard MSMEs to ONDC, with 50% of beneficiaries targeted to be women-owned; a DigiDukaan programme reportedly has over 13,000 kirana stores in Hyderabad and Jaipur. Confidence: medium. — [Cross-Border Magazine](https://cross-border-magazine.com/ondc-500-million-transactions-india/)
- Conversational and AI apps on ONDC: Gupshup's WhatsApp-based buyer app (June 2024, beta, food first; voice and RCS planned, shipping not confirmed); Mystore "Genie" AI agent built on Google Cloud with voice and text product search for buyers (June 2025); ONDC's own "Sahayak" WhatsApp bot for seller education and onboarding (2025; English first, Marathi, Kannada and Tamil promised); a Meta–ONDC partnership to build conversational buyer–seller experiences on WhatsApp. Confidence: high on announcements, low on usage (no numbers published). — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/gupshup-rolls-out-indias-first-conversational-buyer-app-on-ondc); [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/mystore-unveils-mystore-genie-an-ai-agent-built-on-google-cloud-to-supercharge-ondc-network-buyer-journeys); [eGov Elets](https://egov.eletsonline.com/2025/07/ondc-launches-sahayak-whatsapp-bot-to-support-sellers-in-indian-languages/); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/ondc-meta-partner-to-enable-small-biz-unlock-power-of-digital-commerce)
- Beckn and agents: FIDE showed a "Beckn action bot" linking OpenAI models to Beckn (Pramod Varma called it "the trailer of the future"); Sujith Nair argues Beckn can be the contracting layer when buyer and seller agents talk, and notes ONDC seller apps are generally not AI-enabled; Beckn Labs and Google Cloud launched Beckn Onix (3 Nov 2025), an open-source implementation that can use Google's Agent Development Kit; a community open-source Beckn–MCP bridge exists (not an official release). Confidence: medium (statements and demos, not production evidence). — [Swarajya](https://swarajyamag.com/technology/beckn-meets-chatgpt-an-early-look-at-how-we-might-shop-in-the-future); [Analytics India Magazine](https://analyticsindiamag.com/when-two-ai-agents-communicate-beckn-can-be-the-contracting-infrastructure/); [Google Cloud press release](https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/2025-11-03-Beckn-Labs-and-Google-Cloud-Partner-to-Accelerate-the-Adoption-of-Open-Networks-Worldwide-with-Beckn-Onix); [MCP community discussion](https://gh.nn.ci/orgs/modelcontextprotocol/discussions/249) (via search summary)
- Competing agent-commerce standards get more 2026 attention than Beckn: OpenAI/Stripe's Agentic Commerce Protocol and Google/Shopify's Universal Commerce Protocol (announced at NRF 2026). Confidence: medium (commentary). — [Fynd blog, Sep 2026](https://www.fynd.com/blog/agentic-commerce-protocol-acp-what-it-is-and-how-it-works); [Raj Nandan essay](https://rajnandan.com/posts/state-of-agentic-commerce-2026/) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- Could a small seller's agent be an ONDC seller app? Structurally yes: a seller app (seller network participant) is exactly the role that lists a seller's catalog and answers search/select/confirm calls, and a marketplace-type seller app can hold many small sellers. But retail demand on the network is weak and falling since 2024, so being on ONDC is a distribution option, not a source of customers. The practical route is to connect through an existing seller app or technology provider rather than certify as a participant on day one. (Inference from the role structure in the sources above; ONDC's participant onboarding rules and costs were not checked in a primary source.)
- Beckn fits structured catalog items with a fixed price. Negotiated quotes for services (a tailor, a tutor, a caterer) are the part the protocol supports least in practice on ONDC today, which is where a chat agent has more to offer.
- Service categories (mobility, ticketing, logistics) are what worked on ONDC: standardised, price-comparable, high-frequency. Small-seller retail did not, because discovery and discounts drove demand and both were subsidy-funded.

### Gaps
- No 2026 month-by-month retail (non-mobility) order data found; cannot say whether retail recovered.
- No official Beckn or ONDC agent specification or 2026 announcement found; "Beckn v2 for agents" could not be confirmed.
- Current certification cost, time and compliance burden for a new ONDC seller app not verified.
- Usage numbers for Gupshup's, Mystore's or other conversational ONDC apps not published.

## 5. UPI as the payment rail (paying the seller directly, without the agent handling funds)

### Takeaway
UPI lets a seller's agent request payment by sharing the seller's own QR or payment link so money moves bank-to-bank and the agent never holds it; this is free for small sellers and remains free under the new fee framework for those receiving up to Rs 1 lakh a month. Agent-initiated payment on the buyer side is arriving (UPI Circle delegation, Reserve Pay fund blocks, pilots inside AI chat apps, a private protocol live since June 2026), but NPCI's own agent protocol was still unconfirmed as launched in the sources I found.

### Cited Findings
- Scale: UPI processed 24.51 billion transactions worth Rs 29.82 lakh crore in August 2026; over 24,000 crore (240 billion) transactions in FY2025-26; merchant (P2M) payments are about 63% of volume but a smaller share of value. Confidence: high for monthly/annual totals; medium for the 63% (Finance Ministry figure via press). — [Inc42, 15 Sep 2026](https://inc42.com/features/upi-mdr-regime-explained-heres-what-will-be-charged-after-oct-15/); [Cross-Border Magazine](https://cross-border-magazine.com/ondc-500-million-transactions-india/); [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/news/business/upi-fee-why-govt-is-putting-a-price-on-big-merchant-payments) (via search summary)
- Reach: a payments company blog (Feb 2026) cites 491 million individuals and 65 million merchants on UPI. Confidence: medium (secondary, not an RBI/NPCI table). — [Pine Labs blog](https://www.pinelabs.com/blog/what-upi-circle-reveals-about-where-indias-business-payments-are-headed-next) (via search summary)
- End of blanket zero MDR (major change in 2026): a framework announced in September 2026 sets a 0.4% merchant fee on merchant UPI payments above Rs 2,000, capped at Rs 300 per transaction (reached at Rs 75,000); payments of Rs 2,000 or less stay at zero; small vendors receiving up to Rs 1 lakh a month via UPI QR directly into their account stay at zero, and move to the charged category only after exceeding Rs 1 lakh for three consecutive months; person-to-person transfers stay free; merchants may not pass the fee to customers; UPI apps may not charge a platform fee; flat Rs 5 for utilities/railways/telecom/insurance/fuel above Rs 2,000; 0.02% for capital-market payments. Split of the 0.4%: 0.28% issuer bank, 0.08% UPI app, 0.04% acquiring bank (another outlet reports the split as 40% issuer, 30% acquirer, 20% app, 10% partner bank; the two do not match). Confidence: high on the framework's existence and thresholds; medium on the split. — [Inc42 explainer, 15 Sep 2026](https://inc42.com/features/upi-mdr-regime-explained-heres-what-will-be-charged-after-oct-15/); [Business Today, 15 Sep 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/story/upi-mdr-rules-rs12-on-rs3000-rs200-on-rs50000-and-rs300-cap-on-rs75000-payments-check-faqs-555724-2026-09-15)
- Start date is uncertain: originally 15 October 2026; a report dated 13 October 2026 says NPCI deferred the rollout past the festive season, with charges to apply "post-Diwali"; earlier reports said the date would not move. I could not open the deferral article (blocked) or confirm the new date; status as of mid-November 2026 is unverified. Confidence: low on current status. — [Moneylife headline, Oct 2026](https://www.moneylife.in/article/npci-defers-upi-mdr-rollout-amid-festive-season-select-merchant-payments-to-attract-charges-postdiwali/81931.html); [Inc42, 17 Sep 2026](https://inc42.com/features/mdr-on-upi-is-here-but-who-foots-the-bill/)
- Why the fee came: industry estimates the annual cost of running UPI at about Rs 20,000 crore; the highest government subsidy outgo was Rs 3,631 crore in FY2023-24; payments below the Rs 2,000 threshold are reported as more than 95–96% of merchant UPI volume; Bernstein estimates a fee pool of about Rs 22,000 crore by FY28; who monitors the Rs 1 lakh threshold is described as unresolved. Confidence: medium. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/mdr-on-upi-is-here-but-who-foots-the-bill/); [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/news/business/upi-fee-why-govt-is-putting-a-price-on-big-merchant-payments) (via search summary)
- Merchant onboarding is getting easier at the small end: existing QR codes and soundboxes keep working under the new framework; NPCI's new Android ATMs (GFF, Sep 2026) can issue a merchant UPI QR instantly; a dedicated fund for small-merchant acceptance in tier III/IV centres is to be finalised with RBI. Confidence: medium-high. — [Inc42 explainer](https://inc42.com/features/upi-mdr-regime-explained-heres-what-will-be-charged-after-oct-15/); [Inc42 GFF 2026](https://inc42.com/buzz/gff-2026-npci-launches-instant-card-issuing-atms-deepens-ai-push/)
- In-chat payment for small sellers on WhatsApp: one-tap QR sharing and in-app payment for small businesses (Sep 2025); UPI payments inside Meta's Business AI chat promised "soon" (May 2026). Confidence: high on announcement. — [afaqs](https://www.afaqs.com/news/media/whatsapp-sharpens-its-business-play-at-mumbai-summit-with-payments-ai-support-and-ads-in-status-10470432); [Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/05/introducing-business-ai-on-whatsapp-for-small-businesses-in-india/)
- Delegated payments (UPI Circle): a primary user can delegate to up to five secondary users; full delegation is widely reported as capped at Rs 5,000 per transaction and Rs 15,000 a month; partial delegation requires the primary user's approval each time. Confidence: medium-high (consistent across bank/app pages; no primary NPCI circular read). — [Paytm](https://paytm.com/blog/payments/upi/what-is-upi-circle-transaction-limits); [Ujjivan SFB](https://ujjivansfb.bank.in/banking-blogs/banking-services/upi-circle-delegated-payments-how-it-works-limits) (via search summary)
- Reserve Pay lets a customer block funds for several future debits; reported cap Rs 10,000 for up to 90 days. Confidence: medium. — [Fintech News Singapore](https://fintechnews.sg/136592/fintech-india/upi-ai-agents/); [NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/business/ai-agents-could-soon-make-payments-using-upi/story) (via search summary)
- Agent-initiated payments: a 2025 pilot by Razorpay, NPCI and OpenAI tested payments from ChatGPT using UPI Circle and Reserve Pay; in February 2026 Razorpay and NPCI announced UPI agentic payments in Claude for ordering from Zomato, Swiggy and Zepto (secondary source); Pine Labs launched "P3P", an agentic payment protocol on UPI mandates, on 11 June 2026 and says it is live, with merchant catalogs exposed to agents and Gullak as first partner. Confidence: medium (press releases and secondary coverage). — [Forkast](https://forkast.news/india-eyes-ai-driven-upi-payments-as-npci-weighs-agent-protocol/); [Stellagent](https://stellagent.ai/insights/razorpay-npci-agentic-upi); [Pine Labs press release](https://www.pinelabs.com/media-analyst/the-ai-agent-can-now-pay-pine-labs-launches-p3p-indias-first-agentic-payment-protocol-built-on-upi); [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/pine-labs-launches-p3p-agentic-payment-protocol-on-upi) (via search summary)
- NPCI's own "Unified Agent Protocol": Reuters-sourced reports (1 Sep 2026) said NPCI was preparing a framework, building on UPI Circle and Reserve Pay, to let AI agents make small payments within user-set limits and might unveil it at Global Fintech Fest (8–11 Sep 2026, Mumbai). Coverage of the event confirms NPCI unveiled "AtOM" (an agentic orchestration and messaging platform for UPI integration and partner onboarding, with signed machine-readable audit trails) but describes the agent protocol only as "expected". I found no confirmation of a live launch, limits or liability rules. Confidence: medium that it is planned; low on status. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/npci-to-launch-agentic-payments-on-upi-report/); [Inc42 GFF 2026, 9 Sep 2026](https://inc42.com/buzz/gff-2026-npci-launches-instant-card-issuing-atms-deepens-ai-push/); [Deccan Chronicle/Reuters](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/amp/technology/india-preparing-framework-to-let-ai-agents-make-small-upi-payments-without-approval-1983986)

### Inferences
- A seller-side agent does not need any of the agentic-payment features. It only needs to send the seller's own UPI QR or payment link with the amount filled in and then confirm receipt; money goes buyer bank to seller bank and the agent is never a payment intermediary. Confirmation of receipt is the hard part: without a payment-aggregator integration, the agent cannot see the seller's bank credit and must rely on the seller confirming or on a screenshot (which is fraud-prone). (Inference; UPI deep-link and collect mechanics were not checked in an NPCI primary source in this research.)
- Under the new fee rules, a home seller taking under Rs 1 lakh a month, or selling items under Rs 2,000, pays nothing; a tailor or caterer with Rs 5,000–20,000 tickets who crosses Rs 1 lakh a month for three months would pay 0.4% (Rs 20–80 per such order). This is small but creates an incentive to stay on personal accounts and P2P transfers, which is already common among micro sellers.
- Delegated limits (Rs 5,000 per payment, Rs 15,000 a month) fit small everyday purchases from local sellers; they would matter only if the product also builds a buyer-side agent.

### Gaps
- Whether the UPI merchant fee is actually in force as of 17 Nov 2026, and the revised start date, could not be confirmed.
- No primary NPCI document read for UPI Circle limits, intent-link rules, or merchant onboarding (KYC) categories; these are from bank/app explainers.
- UPI Lite, AutoPay mandate limits and "conversational UPI" (Hello UPI) were not researched in this pass.
- October 2026 UPI volumes not found.

## 6. How consumers ask for quotes and compare local providers; what sellers pay for leads

### Takeaway
Local discovery runs on paid lead platforms (Justdial, IndiaMART, Sulekha) and managed marketplaces (Urban Company), and the provider-side complaints are consistent: annual prepaid packages, the same lead sold to several providers, fake or junk leads, and high commissions. Hard independent data on lead prices is scarce; most numbers are from blogs with a commercial interest.

### Cited Findings
- Indicative prices (vendor/comparison blogs, not independent): IndiaMART subscriptions from about Rs 25,000–30,000 a year; Justdial packages about Rs 15,000 to Rs 50,000+ a year depending on city and category; an illustrative Justdial cost of Rs 300–800 per lead in one category guide. Confidence: low-medium. — [Vedain blog](https://vedain.com/blog/indiamart-vs-justdial-which-lead-source-works-better-for-indian-smbs); [QuickEstimate blog](https://quickestimate.co/blog/solar-lead-from-justdial) (via search summary)
- Complaints: users allege IndiaMART leads are distributed to multiple subscribers and are sometimes already closed when bought; Justdial complainants report "only two leads" and fake calls after paying on recurring charges; a Bengaluru district consumer commission held Justdial liable for deficiency of service for failing to provide leads to a coaching institute that had paid. Confidence: medium (self-reported complaints plus one legal ruling). — [ConsumerComplaints.in](https://www.consumercomplaints.in/indiamart-fraud-and-fake-leads-c3504973); [Voxya complaint, Apr 2026](https://voxya.com/consumer-complaints/false-advertisement-and-looted/263016); [LiveLaw](https://livelaw.in/consumer-cases/bangalore-district-commission-holds-justdial-liable-for-failure-to-provide-customer-leads-despite-receiving-payment-by-coaching-institute-249801) (via search summary)
- Urban Company (managed marketplace, fixed prices rather than quotes): after protests in Oct 2021 it cut the top commission to 25% (from 30–35% depending on report) and capped monthly penalties at Rs 3,000 (to fall to Rs 1,500); partners reported joining fees of about Rs 20,000 (beauticians) to Rs 50,000, and a 2021 "minimum guarantee" plan with subscription fees from Rs 2,000 in return for jobs; further protests in 2023; the company sued protest leaders in Dec 2021. Confidence: medium-high (multiple press reports; figures vary). — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/business/urban-company-cuts-commission-to-25-from-30-after-workers-protest); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/in-depth/the-urban-cost-of-gig-service); [Scroll](https://scroll.in/magazine/1014700/were-being-pushed-into-poverty-voices-of-women-who-took-on-the-unicorn-start-up-urban-company) (via search summary)
- A side-industry has grown around lead platforms: tools that auto-call or auto-reply to IndiaMART/Justdial leads "within a minute" (2026 vendor posts), which signals that speed of first response to a shared lead is treated as decisive by providers. Confidence: low-medium (vendor content). — [Edesy blog](https://edesy.in/blog/call-indiamart-justdial-leads-ai-voice-2026.md) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- Because the same lead goes to several providers, the first credible reply with a price tends to win; this is the clearest case where an agent that answers and quotes instantly has measurable value for a local service provider, and where the provider is already paying real money (tens of thousands of rupees a year) for leads.
- Consumers have no neutral way to get several comparable quotes from local independents; they either take a platform's fixed price (Urban Company) or submit one enquiry and receive calls from many sellers. A buyer-side "get me three quotes" agent talking to seller agents addresses a real gap, but only works once enough sellers are reachable by an agent.

### Gaps
- No independent data on cost per lead, lead-to-order conversion, or provider churn on Justdial, IndiaMART or Sulekha; Sulekha pricing not found.
- No data on how many quotes Indian consumers typically collect for a local service, or by which channel.
- Current (2025–2026) Urban Company commission structure not verified.

## 7. Language and literacy: vernacular and voice

### Takeaway
The direction is clear and uncontested (most Indian internet users prefer Indian languages; voice notes are heavily used; large platforms are shipping voice-first, vernacular ordering in 2025–2026), but I found no rigorous measurement of how much commerce chat is in Indian languages or by voice note, nor a controlled study showing adoption lift from vernacular or voice assistance.

### Cited Findings
- Product moves by large players (evidence of where they see demand): Meta's Business AI supports "all native Indian languages" (May 2026); Flipkart launched a voice-enabled WhatsApp bot with Sarvam AI for small retailers to place wholesale orders in regional languages (2026); Meesho said it is betting on voice-led commerce for its next phase of AI adoption (Mar 2026); ONDC's Sahayak seller bot is adding Marathi, Kannada and Tamil; Andhra Pradesh's "Mana Mitra" WhatsApp chatbot offers 700+ services in multiple languages and has been used by 4 million citizens (Sep 2025). Confidence: high on launches. — [Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/05/introducing-business-ai-on-whatsapp-for-small-businesses-in-india/); [MIT Sloan Management Review Middle East](https://www.mitsloanme.com/?p=9740); [CIO Elets, 26 Mar 2026](https://cio.eletsonline.com/?p=75843); [eGov Elets](https://egov.eletsonline.com/2025/07/ondc-launches-sahayak-whatsapp-bot-to-support-sellers-in-indian-languages/); [afaqs](https://www.afaqs.com/news/media/whatsapp-sharpens-its-business-play-at-mumbai-summit-with-payments-ai-support-and-ads-in-status-10470432)
- Voice notes in regional languages are common among Indian WhatsApp users, and WhatsApp's transcription feature did not cover those languages at the time of the analyst note. Confidence: medium (analyst blog, qualitative). — [QKS Group](https://qksgroup.com/blogs/a-step-towards-accessible-communication-with-whatsapp-voice-message-transcripts-913) (via search summary)
- Bain/Meta (Oct 2023 survey): about 90% of "non-savvy" digital users prefer to deal with SMBs through conversational platforms, i.e. chat is the on-ramp for less digitally fluent buyers. Confidence: medium (Meta-commissioned). — [Meta Newsroom, May 2024](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/05/genai-powered-conversational-commerce-to-fuel-business-growth-in-india/)
- Seller-side digital divide: only 9.4% of rural female-owned informal establishments use the internet versus 40.6% of urban male-owned (ASUSE 2023-24); lack of skills is the main obstacle reported by home-based women sellers (LEAD, 2024). Confidence: medium-high. — [CEDA Ashoka](https://ceda.ashoka.edu.in/from-ownership-to-operations-mapping-gender-inequality-in-indias-informal-enterprises/); [LEAD/IFMR](https://ifmrlead.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Harnessing-the-Power-of-Social-Commerce-Report-April-2024.pdf) (via search summary)
- Unverified vendor claims, listed so the writer can avoid them: "over 55% of Indian internet users use voice search regularly"; "40% of smartphone users are more comfortable speaking than typing"; "40% of rural India relies on voice search (Nielsen 2023)"; "WhatsApp carries 7 billion voice notes a day, India leads". None traced to a primary source. Confidence: low. — [Boat blog](https://www.boat-lifestyle.com/blogs/blog/voice-ai-in-vernacular-how-ai-is-making-smart-audio-more-conversational-for-indias-diverse-users); [msg10x blog](https://msg10x.duckdns.org/blog/introducing-voice-commerce) (via search summary)
- Known technical weakness: mid-sentence Hindi–English code-switching is described by vendors as a failure point for voice bots. Confidence: low-medium (vendor statement). — [Bot.space blog](https://www.bot.space/blog/ai-voice-agents-hindi-regional-languages) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- A seller agent for this segment must read and answer voice notes and mixed-language text (for example Hindi written in Latin script) from customers, and must take the owner's instructions and confirmations by voice; text-only English would exclude most of the informal base.
- Since Meta, Flipkart and Meesho now all ship vernacular or voice AI for small merchants, language support is a minimum requirement, not a differentiator.

### Gaps
- No measured share of business chats in Indian languages or as voice notes.
- No controlled or before/after study on adoption or conversion lift from vernacular or voice-first assistance for small sellers.
- The Google–KPMG Indian-languages study and IAMAI–Kantar internet reports were not opened in this pass; current Indian-language internet user shares are not cited here.
