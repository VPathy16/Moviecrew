# Dayone Crew — a ready-made vanara team that onboards every new hire for you
Lens: Wedge: onboarding new employees with a ready-made vanara team
## Customer & pain
Buyer: Head of People/IT at 100-2,000 person companies with steady hiring (agencies, SaaS, clinics groups, logistics, BPOs). New hires take 3-8 weeks to be productive; HR/IT/managers burn hours on account setup, policy questions, paperwork chasing, "who do I ask" pings, and buddy check-ins. Existing tools (Workday, Rippling, BambooHR checklists) track tasks but don't do them or sit with the hire.
## Product
Each new hire gets a personal team on day -7 through day 90:
- Phone: a "Buddy" vanara (voice/chat) handles pre-boarding: documents, bank/tax forms, first-day logistics, reminders, "where is X" questions, 30/60/90 check-ins with the manager.
- Cloud: an "IT-setup" vanara files access requests, tracks approvals, and keeps working overnight while devices are off; an "HR-ops" vanara chases signatures and updates the HRIS.
- Desktop: a "Coach" vanara drives the browser/apps to walk the hire through tools (e.g. fills the expense tool alongside them, sets up the CRM views), inside company rules.
Shared memory holds the hire's progress, blockers, and what they asked; agent-to-agent handover means Buddy hears "I can't access the repo" and hands to IT-setup, which reports back to Buddy. Managers and HR get a live readiness view, not a checklist. Personal data (ID, bank) stays on-device via reference tokens; approvals and spend rules are enforced locally.
## Wedge (first product, first 10 customers)
"Day-One Buddy": phone-only voice/chat agent plus a playbook builder that turns a company's existing onboarding doc into scoped vanaras in a day. Sell to 10 companies of 100-300 staff hiring 5+/month, via founder's network, HR communities, and staffing/agency owners. Price as a pilot on the next 5 hires.
## Enterprise path
Start per-hire; expand to internal mobility, role changes, and offboarding (same team, different playbook); then permanent per-employee teams. SSO/SCIM, HRIS and ITSM connectors (via the MCP pipeline), audit logs, data-residency, and per-role tool scopes unlock security review at larger firms.
## Business model & pricing
Per-hire onboarding fee (~$150-300/hire, includes 90 days), then ~$8-15/employee/month for the persistent team. BYO or bundled model key. Target ACV $15-40K for mid-market.
## Why big tech (Google/Apple/Microsoft/OpenAI/Anthropic) won't just do it
Horizontal assistants ship generic copilots; onboarding needs per-company playbooks, HR/IT integrations, and a cross-surface memory that follows a hire from personal phone to work laptop, which is messy channel work with low glory. Microsoft may bundle a Viva-style feature, but not cross-vendor and not phone-first for pre-day-one hires who have no corporate account yet.
## Biggest risk
Buyers treat onboarding as a checklist feature within their HRIS, and pre-boarding on a personal phone raises privacy/consent concerns; sales cycles for HR budgets can be slow. (Pricing and time-saved figures are guesses.)
## Uses founder's existing assets
Scoped agents with locked tools (Buddy/IT/HR roles), agent handover, scheduled background jobs (day-N nudges), on-device approvals and spend rules, reference-token privacy, MCP tool-role classification for connecting HRIS/ITSM, voice, BYO model key. New: cloud runner and desktop agent.
