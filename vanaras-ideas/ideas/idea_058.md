# Handoff Crew — a team of agents that carries a freelance designer's client work from phone to cloud to desktop
Lens: Designers
## Customer & pain
Freelance designers and 2-10 person design studios (brand, UI, packaging, motion). They pay themselves. Their week is lost to non-design work: client feedback arriving by WhatsApp, email, Figma comments and voice notes; chasing approvals; version naming; export and packaging; invoicing; "can you just resize this for 14 formats". Feedback is scattered, so revisions are missed and scope creeps unpaid.
## Product
- Phone vanara (Intake): captures client messages, voice notes and screenshots, turns them into a structured change list per project, and nudges the client for sign-off. Personal and client data is tokenised, so the model never sees raw client contact details.
- Cloud vanara (Ops): runs 24/7 on the project. It keeps the brief, revision log and scope ledger. It drafts the change-order when feedback exceeds the agreed rounds, and it prepares invoices and reminders.
- Desktop vanara (Production): drives Figma, Illustrator, Photoshop and Finder. It does resizes, exports, file naming, asset packaging and uploads to the client's drive. Each step runs under scoped tools and on-device approvals.
- Shared memory holds each client's taste, brand rules, rounds used and past decisions. Agent-to-agent handoff: Intake logs "client wants the logo larger" -> Ops checks scope -> Production executes on the laptop when it is next on -> Intake tells the client a preview is ready. Agents work while the laptop is off, queuing desktop jobs.
## Wedge (first product, first 10 customers)
"Feedback-to-changelist": forward a client's messy feedback (WhatsApp, voice, email) to the phone vanara and get a clean numbered revision list plus a scope-ledger warning. Sell to 10 freelancers found via design communities (Dribbble, r/graphic_design, design Discords), at $19/mo. Add the export/resize desktop job as the second step.
## Enterprise path
In-house design teams and agencies: an agent team per designer under company rules (brand-asset access, approval limits, client data residency), with an audit log of agent actions and studio-level shared memory of brand guidelines. Sold per seat to studio managers and ops leads, later to marketing departments.
## Business model & pricing
Subscription: Solo $19-29/mo, Studio $49/seat/mo, BYO model key discount. Usage-based add-on for cloud hours. Enterprise seat plus admin/audit tier.
## Why big tech (Google/Apple/Microsoft/OpenAI/Anthropic) won't just do it
Adobe and Figma are the closest, but they stay inside their own canvas and do not handle WhatsApp/voice client channels. Big platforms build horizontal assistants; a scope-and-revision ledger for designer-client relationships across surfaces is too vertical and too messy for them. Cross-tool phone-cloud-desktop handoff with per-agent tool locks is the moat, not any single feature.
## Biggest risk
Desktop automation of Adobe/Figma is brittle (guess: UI changes and plugin limits), and Figma or Adobe could ship comparable agents. Freelancers also have low willingness to pay and churn.
## Uses founder's existing assets
Scoped vanaras with locked tools, agent handover, scheduled background jobs, on-device approvals and spend rules (change-orders and invoices), reference-token privacy for client data, MCP pipeline classifying tools by role (Figma, drive, invoicing), voice notes input, BYO model key. New: cloud and desktop surfaces.
