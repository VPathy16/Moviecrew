# Tolka — a per-employee agent team that works in the employee's own language and hands work across languages
Lens: Language-diverse markets (multilingual agents)
## Customer & pain
Payer: operations heads at mid-size multilingual firms (Indian, Gulf, SE Asian and African logistics, manufacturing, real-estate and healthcare-staffing companies, 200-5,000 staff). Frontline and mid-level staff work in Hindi, Tamil, Arabic, Tagalog, Swahili or code-mixed speech (Hinglish), while ERPs, contracts and managers' reports are in English. Work gets lost in translation: voice notes on WhatsApp never reach the system of record, English-only copilots go unused, and managers re-type or mistranslate.
## Product
Each employee gets a team of vanaras that speaks their language and code-mix.
- Phone: voice-first agent takes a voice note or call in Tamil ("truck 4 delayed, customs hold"), logs it, sets reminders, and replies in Tamil.
- Cloud: a project agent works 24/7, turning notes into tickets, English status reports and vendor emails in the vendor's language.
- Desktop: an agent fills the English ERP/CRM forms and files, checking each field against the original utterance.
Shared memory holds the employee's glossary (their own terms, place names, product nicknames), with agent-to-agent sync so a Tamil-speaking supervisor's phone agent hands a task to the Arabic-speaking dispatcher's agent, each side seeing its own language plus an audit-grade original. The company-wide glossary is learned once and shared under company rules.
## Wedge (first product, first 10 customers)
A Tolka phone app for field supervisors: voice-note-to-structured-report in 5 regional languages plus English summary emailed to the manager. Ship to 10 small logistics and construction firms the founder can reach through his network, paid per seat monthly, BYO model key at first to keep costs near zero.
## Enterprise path
Add the desktop agent for ERP entry, then the cloud project agents. Then admin console: per-role tool scoping, approval and spend rules enforced on device, on-prem or regional data residency, glossary governance, SSO. Reference tokens mean personal and customer data never reaches the model, which helps in Gulf and Indian data-protection regimes.
## Business model & pricing
Per-seat SaaS, about $12 per frontline seat and $35 per office seat monthly, plus a usage pass-through for voice minutes. Enterprise contracts with residency and glossary governance at $50-150K per year. (Pricing is a guess.)
## Why big tech (Google/Apple/Microsoft/OpenAI/Anthropic) won't just do it
They ship global, English-first, horizontal assistants; long-tail dialect and code-mix quality, per-company glossaries and cross-language handoffs are unglamorous per-market work. Their copilots also live inside their own suites, not across a customer's WhatsApp, local ERPs and legacy tools. Model-agnostic BYO key lets Tolka ride whichever model is best per language.
## Biggest risk
Model quality on low-resource dialects and noisy audio may be uneven, and errors that get written into an ERP are costly; also WhatsApp/telephony access limits and sales-cycle length in multiple markets.
## Uses founder's existing assets
Scoped vanaras with their own tools, handover between agents, scheduled background jobs, on-device approvals and spend rules, reference-token privacy, service-agnostic MCP pipeline (connects arbitrary local ERPs), voice, BYO model key. New: glossary memory, per-language evaluation, desktop and cloud surfaces.
