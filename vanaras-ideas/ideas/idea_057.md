# Specwright — a personal agent team for every product manager that turns customer noise into shipped decisions
Lens: Product managers
## Customer & pain
Individual PMs and their heads of product at 50-2000 person software companies. A PM spends about 60% of the week on synthesis and chasing: reading support tickets, call transcripts and Slack threads; updating PRDs; nudging engineers; writing status for five audiences; reconciling roadmap tool with reality. Insight is lost between the call, the doc and the ticket, and nobody notices when a shipped feature contradicts what customers asked for.
## Product
- Phone vanara (Scout): captures voice notes after customer calls, walks-and-talks, reminders like "ask Priya about the SSO estimate", and approves small actions from anywhere.
- Cloud vanara (Analyst): runs 24/7 on the tracker, support inbox, call recordings and analytics; clusters feedback, drafts PRD deltas, watches metrics after launch, and prepares the Monday brief.
- Desktop vanara (Scribe): works in the PM's own browser and apps (Figma, Notion, Jira, slides), producing artefacts in the PM's house style.
- Shared memory holds the "product belief ledger": each decision, its evidence, owner and expiry date. Agents hand off: Scout hears a churn-risk remark, Analyst finds 14 matching tickets, Scribe opens a PRD amendment for approval. The ledger flags when new evidence contradicts an old decision.
## Wedge (first product, first 10 customers)
Weekly "Evidence Digest" plus voice capture: connect support inbox and call transcripts (Gong/Zoom exports), get a ranked, cited list of what customers are asking, with one-tap PRD amendment drafts. Sell to 10 PMs at seed/Series A startups through PM communities, LinkedIn and Lenny-style newsletters at $40/month self-serve. Founder's own domain of tooling makes demos easy.
## Enterprise path
Team ledger shared across PMs (with per-person vanaras), SSO, tool-role allowlists, spend and approval rules set by the head of product, audit log of every agent action, reference-token privacy so customer PII in tickets never reaches the model, BYO model key or company-hosted endpoint. Expand from product to design and customer success.
## Business model & pricing
$40/PM/month self-serve; team plan $90/seat with shared ledger and admin; enterprise custom (~$150/seat plus platform fee). Model usage passthrough via BYO key or metered credits at cost plus margin.
## Why big tech (Google/Apple/Microsoft/OpenAI/Anthropic) won't just do it
General assistants are horizontal and stateless per vendor suite; PM data lives across neutral tools (Jira, Linear, Gong, Zendesk, Figma) that no single vendor owns. A vertical opinionated workflow with a decision ledger is too narrow for them, and cross-surface, cross-vendor agents with on-device approvals conflict with their platform lock-in.
## Biggest risk
PM is a small, fickle buyer segment and integrations are heavy; Productboard, Linear, Notion and Atlassian may bundle "AI insights" and commoditise the digest. (Guess: PM seat willingness to pay is $30-60/month.)
## Uses founder's existing assets
Scoped vanaras with locked tool sets (Scout/Analyst/Scribe), handover between agents, scheduled background jobs (Monday brief, post-launch watchers), on-device approvals and spend rules, reference-token privacy for customer data, service-agnostic MCP pipeline classifying Jira/Linear/Gong tools by role, voice capture, BYO model key. Android app is the phone surface as-is.
