# Sideline — a private agent team for every coach that runs practice, parents and logistics while the season is on
Lens: Sports teams / coaches
## Customer & pain
Paying customer: the coach or club (youth, high school, amateur, small college), starting with individual coaches paying personally. Coaches are volunteers or underpaid, and lose 10+ hours a week to WhatsApp/SMS threads with parents, rain-outs, carpools, rosters, availability, fees, forms, referee bookings and pitch/field permits. Team apps (TeamSnap, SportsEngine) are schedule bulletin boards; they don't act, and parents still ignore them. Child data makes coaches nervous about pasting into ChatGPT.
## Product
Phone: a coach's "vanara" team: Scheduler (availability, rain-out re-plan), Parent Liaison (drafts and sends messages, chases RSVPs and fees, all approval-gated), Squad Planner (lineups and minutes fairness from attendance and notes), Voice: coach dictates after training and notes become plans. Cloud: agents run 24/7 through the week, e.g. watch weather and field-booking sites, rebook a pitch, nudge unpaid parents on Thursday, prep the match-day pack. Desktop: fills league portals, registration and referee forms, exports rosters to spreadsheets, files expense claims. Shared memory: each player's availability, injuries, parent preferences and coach notes; a handover from Parent Liaison to Scheduler means "three kids out, rebook smaller pitch" happens without the coach relaying it. Child data stays on device behind reference tokens; the model sees "Player_7".
## Wedge (first product, first 10 customers)
Android app "rain-out and RSVP agent": connects to the coach's existing WhatsApp/SMS, calendar and one league portal; handles weekly availability and last-minute changes with approval. Sell to 10 volunteer coaches across UK/US/India/Australia cricket, football and baseball via coaching forums and clubs, free 1 season then $9/month.
## Enterprise path
Club plan (per team seat), then leagues, school districts and academies where every coach gets a team under the club's safeguarding rules (approved recipients, no direct DMs to minors, audit log). Then pro/semi-pro analysts and staff.
## Business model & pricing
Coach $9-15/month BYO key or bundled; club $5 per team/month plus $200/yr admin; league/district contracts $5-20K/yr. Extra: paid fee-collection take (payments partner).
## Why big tech (Google/Apple/Microsoft/OpenAI/Anthropic) won't just do it
Niche, seasonal, fragmented across sports and leagues, with safeguarding and minors' data liability nobody wants; horizontal assistants won't build league-portal integrations or per-sport workflows. Incumbents (TeamSnap) lack agent tech but hold distribution, so they are the partner/acquirer, not a blocker.
## Biggest risk
Parents live in WhatsApp, and messaging-platform policy (bots, consent) plus safeguarding rules for minors could limit automation; willingness to pay among volunteer coaches is low. (Guess: retention outside a season is weak.)
## Uses founder's existing assets
Scoped vanaras with own tools, handover, scheduled background jobs, on-device approvals and spend rules (fees, bookings), reference-token privacy for children's data, voice for post-training dictation, MCP pipeline for calendar/weather/booking tools, BYO key.
