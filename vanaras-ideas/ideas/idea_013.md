# Proxy Table — a chief-of-staff agent team for founders that acts in their name, inside rules they set once
Lens: Founders and executives (chief-of-staff agents)
## Customer & pain
Paying customer: seed to Series B founders and startup executives (CEO, COO, VP Sales), about 5-50 person companies, paying personally or expensing $150-300/month. Their pain is a day fragmented across Gmail, Slack, calendar, investor updates, hiring loops, board prep and family logistics. A human chief of staff costs $150K+ and needs a lot of trust. Today's AI assistants draft text but will not act, because founders cannot delegate under an unclear blast radius.
## Product
- Phone: the founder's voice and approval surface. Morning brief, "reschedule the board dinner", approve or reject with one tap. Spend, send and share rules are enforced on the device.
- Cloud: agents that run all night. An investor-update vanara pulls from metrics, a hiring vanara chases scorecards, a follow-up vanara watches every "let's talk next week" thread.
- Desktop: a work vanara operates the apps that have no API (ATS, banking portal, legacy CRM) and prepares board decks from local files.
- Shared memory holds commitments, relationships, preferences and "what I promised whom". Agent-to-agent sync means a promise made on a call (phone) becomes a task in cloud, a draft on desktop, and a reminder back on phone.
- Delegation Policy: a plain-language, auditable rulebook ("may reschedule anything except investors and board; may spend up to $200; never send to press"). Personal data reaches the model only as reference tokens.
## Wedge (first product, first 10 customers)
Commitment Keeper: a phone plus cloud pair that reads mail and calendar, extracts every promise the founder makes and every promise made to them, and closes loops (drafts, nudges, reschedules) under approval. Get 10 founders from the founder's network, YC-style Slack groups and a build-in-public log. Price low, sell "nothing falls through the cracks."
## Enterprise path
Founders become internal champions. Add a team tier: each exec gets their own team of vanaras, while the company sets org-wide policy (which data classes may leave, spend caps, audit export). The natural next buyers are the founder's leadership team, then the whole company under IT, with SSO, SCIM and audit logs.
## Business model & pricing
$99/month solo, $249/month executive tier with desktop vanara, BYO model key discount. Enterprise $60-120 per seat per month plus a policy and audit add-on. Model cost passes through when using BYO key, so margins stay high.
## Why big tech (Google/Apple/Microsoft/OpenAI/Anthropic) won't just do it
Each is locked to its own ecosystem and its own assistant. Founders live across Google, Slack, Apple and Microsoft stacks, and neutral cross-suite delegation with on-device rules is awkward for any single vendor. Liability for autonomous actions on someone's behalf is also something platforms avoid; a small vendor can specialise in it. (Guess: platforms will ship good generic agents; the moat is trust plus policy depth.)
## Biggest risk
Trust and error cost: one wrong email to an investor kills the account. Second, platform agents get good enough for "inbox triage" and commoditise the wedge.
## Uses founder's existing assets
Scoped vanaras with locked tools, handover between agents, on-device approvals and spend rules (the Delegation Policy core), reference-token privacy, scheduled background jobs, the service-agnostic MCP pipeline (mail, calendar, CRM, banking), voice, and BYO model key. New build: cloud runtime, desktop agent, shared memory store.
