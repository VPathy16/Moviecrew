# Keyhand — a three-surface AI team for every solo real estate agent, that never leaks client data
Lens: Real estate agents
## Customer & pain
Independent agents and small brokerages (1-10 agents) in the US/UK/Canada/Australia. They live on their phone (calls, texts, showings), lose leads to slow follow-up (speed-to-lead is the biggest conversion driver), and drown in paperwork: MLS entry, CRM updates, transaction checklists, compliance. Client PII (IDs, finances, offers) sits in a dozen tools. Brokers fear agents pasting client data into ChatGPT (fair housing, privacy law exposure).
## Product
Named vanaras per agent, each scoped to its own tools:
- Phone: Lead vanara answers/returns calls and texts within seconds, books showings, reminds the agent before appointments, logs voice notes after a showing. Approvals and spend rules on-device (e.g. no offer figure sent without a tap).
- Cloud: Transaction vanara runs the deal 24/7: chases lenders, inspectors, title with deadlines, keeps the checklist, drafts listing copy and comps overnight.
- Desktop: Paperwork vanara fills MLS forms, e-sign packets and CRM through the browser/apps where no API exists.
Shared memory + sync: a voice note on the phone ("buyers hated the kitchen, budget really 850k") updates the cloud deal file and desktop-drafted follow-up. Client data stays as reference tokens; the model never sees raw PII.
## Wedge (first product, first 10 customers)
Android "after-showing" vanara: voice note in, then follow-up text to client, CRM update, next-showing reminder, all with one-tap approval. Sell $49/mo to solo agents via real estate Facebook groups, brokerage team leads, local association meetups. Founder can hand-onboard 10 agents. BYO model key lowers cost.
## Enterprise path
Brokerage-level plan: broker sets policy (fair-housing word filters, spend and disclosure rules, audit log per agent, data-residency), sees compliance dashboards, and keeps client data when agents leave. Then franchises and teams with 50-500 agents, and expansion to mortgage brokers and property managers.
## Business model & pricing
Per-agent SaaS: $49 solo, $99 with cloud and desktop vanaras, brokerage $30/seat with 50+ seats plus admin/compliance tier. Usage credits for calling minutes. ~80% gross margin (guess).
## Why big tech (Google/Apple/Microsoft/OpenAI/Anthropic) won't just do it
Horizontal assistants will not build MLS-, transaction-, and brokerage-compliance-specific workflows, or chase 1.5M small agents one at a time. Scoped-agent-with-on-device-approvals is the compliance story brokers need and general assistants do not offer. Existing CRMs (Follow Up Boss, kvCORE) are cloud-only and lack phone/desktop presence.
## Biggest risk
Distribution and trust: agents are relationship-driven and wary of AI speaking as them; MLS and portal terms may restrict automation (guess). Phone-call regulation (TCPA, call recording consent) adds legal overhead.
## Uses founder's existing assets
UNO scoped vanaras and handover (lead to transaction to paperwork), scheduled background jobs (deadline chasing), on-device approvals and spend rules, reference-token privacy layer, MCP pipeline for CRM/calendar/e-sign tools, voice, BYO model key.
