# Troop — your personal AI team is free; the moment you invite one person, it becomes a paid shared team
Lens: Pricing: freemium consumer to team upgrade
## Customer & pain
Payers: 2-10 person groups who already run life or work out of chat threads: freelance duos and micro-studios (designers, consultants, builders), co-founders, and couples running a household plus a side business. Pain today: each person has a private AI assistant that knows nothing about the other's commitments, so shared errands, client follow-ups and deadlines live in WhatsApp/Slack and drop. Team tools (Notion, Asana) demand setup nobody has time for.
## Product
Free: one person's vanara team on phone (reminders, messages, calls, errands), BYO model key, private on-device memory. Paid trigger is invitation: a "shared vanara" that belongs to the group. Phone vanaras capture requests ("chase the invoice", "book the venue"); cloud vanaras keep working the shared project 24/7 (drafts, research, follow-ups); desktop vanaras handle files/browser work on whichever member's machine is on. Shared memory has two layers: private per person (never leaves their device, reference tokens only) and a group layer both people's agents read and write. Agent-to-agent sync lets Priya's phone vanara hand "send the revised quote" to Sam's desktop vanara, with approval and spend rules enforced on the device of whoever owns the tool or money.
## Wedge (first product, first 10 customers)
"Shared vanara for two": Android app, invite by link, one shared task/errand inbox with handover between the two people's agents. First 10: founder's own network of freelancer/couple pairs, plus a build-in-public thread in indie-maker and freelancer communities.
## Enterprise path
Groups of 5-10 become department pilots; each member's private layer stays theirs while a company policy layer (approved tools, spend caps, audit log) is added. Because employees already have Troop teams, procurement is upgrade-in-place: admin console, SSO, data-residency.
## Business model & pricing
Free forever for solo (costs us little: BYO key, on-device). Team plan $8 per member per month for shared memory, cloud vanaras and cross-person handover (cloud compute metered above a fair-use cap, or BYO key). Business tier $20 per seat with policy/audit. Every paid team needs a second member, so the invite is the growth loop (conversion rate is a guess).
## Why big tech (Google/Apple/Microsoft/OpenAI/Anthropic) won't just do it
Their assistants are single-identity by design and their groups products are tied to their own accounts and tool ecosystems. Cross-person, cross-vendor handover with device-enforced approvals and per-person privacy is awkward for them (liability, account model), and small groups are too small a segment to prioritise. Service-agnostic MCP lets Troop sit across Google, Microsoft and Slack.
## Biggest risk
Invite loop fails: the second person must adopt an Android app, and iOS partners break the pair. Mitigate with a web invite path.
## Uses founder's existing assets
Scoped vanaras with locked tools, handover between agents (extended across people), scheduled background jobs (cloud 24/7), on-device approvals and spend rules, reference-token privacy (the private layer), MCP role-classified pipeline, voice, BYO key (keeps free tier cheap).
