# Shepherd Crew — an agent team for the volunteer-run church that never sleeps
Lens: Churches and communities
## Customer & pain
Pays: the church/parish administrator or lead pastor (small-to-mid congregations, 100-2,000 members), later denominations and diocese offices. Pain: ministry runs on 3 paid staff and 60 overstretched volunteers. Rosters, rides to service, hospital-visit follow-ups, prayer requests, giving receipts, new-visitor follow-up and event logistics live in WhatsApp groups, spreadsheets and one person's head. When that person burns out or leaves, the memory leaves too. Existing church-management software (Planning Center, Breeze, etc.) is a database people must feed; nothing acts.
## Product
Every volunteer and staffer gets their own small team of vanaras under a shared congregation memory.
- Phone: a volunteer's "Care" vanara reminds them of visits, drafts and sends check-in messages/calls, logs outcomes by voice. A "Rota" vanara handles swap requests and reminders. Approvals: nothing goes to a member without the leader's rules (e.g. no pastoral message on bereavement without human sign-off).
- Cloud: a "Secretary" vanara runs 24/7: weekly bulletin, visitor follow-up sequences, rota gaps filled, giving-statement season, event sign-ups.
- Desktop: the "Office" vanara drives the church's existing tools (Planning Center, Google Sheets, Canva, email) so no migration is needed.
Shared memory + handover: Care logs "Mrs. Ito's surgery Thursday", the Rota vanara frees her from Sunday duty, the Secretary adds her to the prayer list and the meal-train, the pastor's brief mentions it. Sensitive pastoral data is held as reference tokens on-device; the AI never sees names, health or giving details.
## Wedge (first product, first 10 customers)
"Visitor-to-volunteer follow-up + care rota" on Android/WhatsApp only: first-time visitors get a warm, approved follow-up within 24h and a care volunteer gets reminders. Sell to 10 churches via founder's own network and a pilot free for 3 months, then $49/mo. Cloud/desktop added later.
## Enterprise path
Denominations, dioceses, mission agencies, and later any volunteer-heavy nonprofit or school parent body. Sells on central rules (safeguarding policies, child-protection message rules, audit trail, data residency), SSO, and a per-region admin console. Employee-team concept maps to staff+volunteers.
## Business model & pricing
Per congregation: $49/mo up to 300 members, $149/mo up to 2,000; per-seat $5 for volunteers beyond 10. Denomination licence $2-5/congregation/mo with rollout support. BYO model key discount. (Pricing is a guess.)
## Why big tech (Google/Apple/Microsoft/OpenAI/Anthropic) won't just do it
Tiny per-customer revenue, high trust/pastoral sensitivity, and denominational nuance; they will not build safeguarding-rule policy layers or sell through pastors' networks. Horizontal agents do not enforce per-role tool scoping on-device with tokenised personal data, which is exactly what churches need to trust it.
## Biggest risk
Trust and sensitivity: one badly worded message to a grieving member ends the pilot. Also low willingness to pay and slow, committee-driven purchasing. Mitigation: approvals default to on for pastoral content.
## Uses founder's existing assets
Scoped vanaras with locked tools (Care vs Rota vs Secretary), handover between agents, scheduled background jobs, approvals and spend rules on device, reference-token privacy (pastoral data never reaches the AI), MCP pipeline classifying tools by role (Planning Center, Sheets, WhatsApp/email), voice for logging visits, BYO key.
