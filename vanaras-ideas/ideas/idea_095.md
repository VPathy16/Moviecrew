# Crewpact — a shared team of agents for small agencies, where each client gets its own scoped crew
Lens: Contrarian: agents for teams, not individuals, from day one

## Customer & pain
Owners of 5-30 person service firms (design, marketing, bookkeeping, recruiting, small law and consulting practices) pay. Their work lives in a team's shared context: client promises, deadlines, who said what. Personal agents each hold a private slice, so nothing agrees. Staff paste client data into chat AIs, and owners have no audit trail, no spend limits, and lose everything when someone leaves.

## Product
The unit is the team crew, not the person. Each client or project gets a crew of vanaras with its own scoped tools and shared memory, and staff plug in as members.
- Phone: a member's vanara takes voice notes after client calls, sets reminders, and approves spend with one tap.
- Cloud: the client crew runs 24/7, chasing invoices, drafting weekly status reports, and watching the shared inbox and tracker.
- Desktop: an agent handles files, browser and apps such as Figma and QuickBooks under the firm's rules.
Shared memory holds one client-scoped truth: commitments, tone, decisions. Agent-to-agent handover means a call promise captured on a phone becomes a cloud task and then a desktop deliverable, with no human retyping. Approvals and spend caps are enforced on the device. Reference tokens keep client PII away from the model. Offboarding a person leaves the client crew and its memory intact.

## Wedge (first product, first 10 customers)
"Client crew" for boutique marketing and design agencies. It starts as a call-to-commitments loop: a phone agent captures promises from client calls, the cloud agent tracks them and sends a Monday status draft, and the owner approves it. Sell to 10 agencies through the founder's network and small-agency communities, at a $99/month pilot, with BYO model key to keep COGS near zero.

## Enterprise path
Per-client crews map onto departments and projects. Add SSO, admin policy packs, audit export, and per-team model allowlists. Because policy is enforced on-device and personal data is tokenised, a security review is easier than for cloud-only agent suites. Mid-market comes after agencies, via team leads who already run crews.

## Business model & pricing
Per-crew pricing of $99-$299/month plus $15/seat/month; usage passes through on BYO key. At 300 agencies averaging $250/month, ARR is about $900K. Enterprise is a per-seat platform license with policy features.

## Why big tech (Google/Apple/Microsoft/OpenAI/Anthropic) won't just do it
Their agents are per-user assistants tied to their own ecosystems. Client-scoped crews spanning Android, cloud and desktop across mixed tool stacks, with model neutrality, are off their roadmap. It is a vertical workflow, and it competes with none of their core products.

## Biggest risk
Agencies are cheap and churny, and Microsoft Copilot or Notion agents could bundle "good enough" team memory. Guess: agency budgets for AI are small, and the trust barrier to letting agents message clients is high.

## Uses founder's existing assets
- Scoped vanaras with locked tools become per-client crews.
- Handover between agents becomes the phone-to-cloud-to-desktop commitment flow.
- Scheduled background jobs power the 24/7 status and invoice chasing.
- On-device approvals and spend rules are the governance layer.
- Reference tokens keep client PII from the model.
- The MCP role-classified pipeline connects agency tools without custom work.
- Voice covers call capture.
- BYO key keeps costs low.
New builds: a cloud runtime, a desktop agent, and a team memory store with multi-user sync.
