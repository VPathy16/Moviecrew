# SiteCrew — a team of scoped AI agents that keeps each site engineer's RFIs, punch lists and daily logs moving, on their phone, in the cloud and on their desktop
Lens: Construction and field engineering
## Customer & pain
Paying customer: specialty subcontractors and small-to-mid general contractors (20-300 staff) doing MEP, civil, solar/EPC and telecom build-outs. Site engineers and foremen spend 1-2 hours a day retyping the same facts: daily logs, RFIs, submittal chasing, photo-tagged punch lists, timesheets, and WhatsApp/SMS threads with the GC and suppliers. Info lives in phones, Procore/Autodesk exports, Excel and email; nothing stays in sync. Late RFIs and undocumented changes cause disputed change orders, which is where the money is lost.
## Product
- Phone (field): voice-first agent. The foreman talks or sends photos; the "Log" vanara builds the daily log, tags photos to drawings/locations, and drafts an RFI. Works offline; syncs later.
- Cloud (projects): a "Chaser" vanara runs 24/7. It chases open RFIs and submittals, watches delivery emails, and compiles weekly progress and change-order evidence packs while devices are off.
- Desktop (office): a "Paperwork" vanara drives Procore/Excel/PDF markups and files the pay-app backup.
- Shared memory and agent-to-agent sync: the field's "concrete pour delayed by rain" becomes a schedule-impact note, then a draft change-order claim with the dated photos, without anyone re-entering it. Each vanara has only its own tools (the Log agent cannot send money or email the owner), and approvals gate anything contractual.
## Wedge (first product, first 10 customers)
Voice-to-daily-log plus RFI drafter as an Android/iOS app, sold at $30 per foreman per month. It is the "WhatsApp-to-Procore" bridge. First 10: solar/EPC and electrical subs found through contractor Facebook groups, trade associations and LinkedIn. Offer a free 2-week trial on one live project. Founder can demo from UNO's voice and background-job stack.
## Enterprise path
Per-project team templates, then company rules: approval limits per role, retention and audit trail (useful in disputes), SSO, and integrations with Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud and Primavera. Sell bottom-up from foremen to the project-controls head, then to a company licence for all field staff.
## Business model & pricing
$30-40 per field user per month; office "Chaser + Paperwork" agents at $150 per project per month; enterprise at $60 per seat with an audit add-on. Roughly 60 to 70 percent gross margin with BYO model key as an option.
## Why big tech (Google/Apple/Microsoft/OpenAI/Anthropic) won't just do it
Horizontal assistants will not build offline site workflows, trade-specific claim evidence, or integrations with each contractor's messy stack. Procore and Autodesk may add AI but are single-vendor and will not touch WhatsApp, supplier email, and a subcontractor's own spreadsheets. It is too niche and too legally sensitive for big tech to prioritise.
## Biggest risk
Trust and liability: a wrong claim or missed notice deadline has real cost, and adoption on site is habit-driven. Also Procore's built-in AI (guess) could commoditise the logging step.
## Uses founder's existing assets
Scoped vanaras with locked tools; handover between agents (log to RFI to claim); scheduled background jobs (chasers); on-device approvals and spend rules; reference tokens so drawings, prices and client names never reach the model; the MCP pipeline for Procore, email and storage; voice; BYO key.
