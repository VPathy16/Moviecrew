# v1 — after loop 1

## Verdict on the thesis
False as stated, true for one product shape. A consumer general assistant app IS closed (distribution owned
by Google/Apple/Meta/OpenAI). But all three constraints were applied only to consumer apps; the founder's
rarest asset is not the app but the safety layer underneath it. The missing thing is customer conversations,
not a miracle.

## Asset ranking (honest)
- Commodity: the UNO app, voice, BYO key, Swiggy integration (6-month edge).
- Rarest: MCP tool-role classifier + per-service privacy gateway; "AI plans, phone executes" with on-device
  approvals, spending rules and data minimisation; scoped sub-agents that can't exceed their tools.

## Best current answer: the EU DMA Android opening
- 16 Jul 2026: EU binding decision (DMA Art. 6(7)) — Google must give rival AI assistants Gemini-equal access to
  ~11 Android capabilities (incl. screen automation, AppFunctions). The 5 most sensitive require CERTIFICATION on:
  user-intent reconfirmation before sensitive actions, minimising inadvertent data disclosure, hardening against
  agentic risks. Eligibility: ≥50k monthly EU users. Draft terms 1 Feb 2027, final 1 May 2027, Android 18 ~1 Aug 2027.
  (Secondary sources; primary text not yet checked.)
- These criteria describe UNO's architecture almost exactly.
- Candidate product: "Certified-ready" — an open-source Android agent safety SDK (intent reconfirmation, data
  minimisation/reference tokens, scoped tool permissions, on-device audit log) + a reference assistant that passes,
  + paid certification-readiness work for challenger assistants (Mistral, Perplexity, OpenAI, Anthropic), OEMs
  (Samsung etc.) and MDM vendors.
- Why big tech can't own it: Google is the gatekeeper arguing against access; rivals need a neutral, auditable layer.
- Global not India; not crowded (no "DMA-certification" vendor found — unverified); not fighting big tech, selling
  to its challengers.

## Alternatives still alive
- H1 model-neutral MCP permission/privacy gateway for companies — CROWDED (Runlayer, AIR $50M seed, Noma, WitnessAI…).
  Gap: none do mobile/on-device.
- H2 consent/spend-rule SDK for commerce/fintech MCP providers — few slow buyers; payment-mandate standards (AP2,
  Visa/Mastercard agent tokens) may absorb it.
- H3 on-device agent for confidentiality-bound professions (lawyers, journalists) — uncrowded on mobile, unproven pay.

## Biggest risks
Big labs build it in-house; criteria undefined until 2027; solo founder credibility with labs/OEMs; the DMA
opening is EU-only unless copied (UK/Japan/Korea/Brazil).

## Cheap test
15 conversations in 3 weeks with teams building agents/MCP servers/Android assistants: is gating/privacy/
certification a painful, budgeted problem?
