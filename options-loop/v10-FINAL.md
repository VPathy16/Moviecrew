# v10 — FINAL (after 10 loops of red team + market research)

## Answer to "we are literally out of options except for a miracle"
You're out of consumer-app options — stop building UNO as a product. You are not out of options.
From India, ~2.5 days/week of contracting covers your costs with room to spare, so you don't need a miracle to
keep going. What you have that others lack is a working gate for the moment an agent sends, shares personal
data, deletes or changes things on someone's behalf. For 13 weeks, try to sell that judgement as reviews to teams
wiring agents into their apps. Likely outcome: modest — solid contract income, and either a small paid niche or
a strong portfolio for a remote agent-safety role. The real risk isn't the market; it's building instead of selling.

## How the idea evolved
v0 out of options → v1 the safety layer, not the app, is the asset → v2 EU DMA forces Google to open Android to
rival assistants (certification) → v3 test agent behaviour on Android → v4 back to a product: AgentGuard runtime →
v5 it's a feature, not a company; action firewall → v6 web agent-trust is owned; mobile gap real but early →
v7 cut to one person's 90 days → v8 sell reviews of agent surfaces that ship today → v9 India-based rupee runway
+ one named company kernel → v10 cut by a third; fact-checked.

## The plan (29 Sep – 31 Dec 2026), ~20 h/week on the company half
1. Runway engine (capped at ~2.5 days/week): remote-from-India Android + AI-agent contract at a realistic
   $35–50/h (≈ ₹2.5–3L/month). If no contract by week 4, take the best offer, even full-time; company half drops
   to ~12 h/week and the test moves to 31 Mar.
2. Ten buyer conversations BEFORE more code. Warm buyers with real action-capable MCP servers: Swiggy, Zepto,
   Razorpay, CRED (not PhonePe — docs-only). Pitch: "agent-readiness review of your MCP" — what an agent can do
   without confirmation (send, share personal data, delete, change address/cart) and injection tests; include
   readiness for NPCI's Unified Agent Protocol delegation. Payment caps themselves are being built into the rails
   (UPI Reserve Pay, UAP, AtOM) — don't sell those.
3. Two paid reviews at ₹1–2.5L (one free for a named testimonial if needed). Global €2–3.5k only after testimonials.
4. One post: "What free scanners miss" — side-by-side with Snyk Agent Scan / Cisco mcp-scanner on 3 open-source
   servers run locally, focused on non-payment actions + an Android screen-injection before/after demo.
5. Code only what a review or the demo needs (small CLI + demo from UNO's classifier, privacy gateway, approvals).

Legal: only open-source servers run locally or targets inside published bug-bounty scopes; 90-day disclosure.
Cut: YC W27 (apply S27 ~March with evidence), EF, full AgentGuard extraction, bounty targets, DMA work, standards,
consumer apps.
