## First milestone is deliberately small

Begin with one four-shot Everest sequence comparing baseline and revised direction with the same provider, settings and attempt cap. Review action completion, causal order, state continuity and editability. Retain rejected attempts and repair costs. The twelve-brief suite is the later expansion; no improvement claim before observed results.

## Problem

There is no controlled evidence yet that MovieCrew improves coherent footage yield over manual workflows.

## Scope

Create 12 briefs covering one-person action, interactions, handoffs, costume, camera movement and dialogue. Define fixed attempts/budgets and human review; separate matched-model from best-workflow comparisons.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Retain every attempt, prompt, input manifest, cost and rejection reason.
- [ ] Report accepted seconds per dollar, beat completion, continuity defects and human repair time.
- [ ] Publish a baseline without fabricated scores; paid runs have a separately authorized cap.
